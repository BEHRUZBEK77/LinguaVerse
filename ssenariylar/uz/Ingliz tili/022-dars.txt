Ingliz tili. 22-dars. Joy predloglari: qayerda?

Assalomu alaykum! Yigirma ikkinchi darsga xush kelibsiz. Oldingi darsda olmoshlarni o‘rgandik: he, her, mine, theirs. Bugun narsa qayerda turganini aytishni o‘rganamiz: in, on, under, behind, between, next to. “Where is my phone?” deb so‘raymiz va rasmni tasvirlaymiz. Dars oxirida xonangizdagi sakkizta narsaning joyini ingliz tilida ayta olasiz.

Joyni bildirishda ikkala til boshqacha ishlaydi. O‘zbek tilida ot oldin, joy so‘zi keyin keladi: “stol ustida”, “xonada”, “divan orqasida”. Bu joy so‘zlari otdan keyin turadi, shuning uchun ularni ko‘makchi yoki qo‘shimcha deymiz. Ingliz tilida esa teskari: joy so‘zi oldin, ot keyin. “On the table”, “in the room”, “behind the sofa”. Bunday so‘zlarni predloglar deymiz.

Birinchi predlog: “in”. U biror narsaning ichida ekanini bildiradi. Quti ichida, sumka ichida, xona ichida: in the box, in the bag, in the room. O‘zbek tilida bu “-da” qo‘shimchasiga to‘g‘ri keladi: sumkada, xonada, muzlatkichda. Shunday qilib, agar narsa ichida bo‘lsa va uni yopiq joy deb tasavvur qilsangiz, “in” ishlating. Shaharlar va mamlakatlar ham “in” bilan keladi: in Tashkent, in London.

Ikkinchi predlog: “on”. U biror narsaning sirtida, ustida ekanini bildiradi va unga tegib turadi. On the table, on the floor, on the shelf. Devorga osilgan rasm ham devorga tegib turadi, shuning uchun “on the wall”. O‘zbek tilida “ustida”, “polda”, “devorda” kabi bir necha shaklga to‘g‘ri keladi. Esda tuting: in ichida, on sirtida.

Birinchi tipik xato: “in the table”. O‘zbekcha “stolda” degan so‘zdagi “-da” ni “in” deb o‘ylab qo‘yamiz. Lekin kitob stolning ichida emas, sirtida turibdi, shuning uchun “on the table”. Teskarisi ham uchraydi: “on the room”. Xona narsalarni ichiga oladi, shuning uchun “in the room”. Har safar o‘zingizga savol bering: u narsa ichidami yoki sirtidami? Javob predlogni tanlab beradi.

Uchinchi predlog: “under”. U biror narsaning tagida, pastida ekanini bildiradi. Under the table, under the bed, under the chair. O‘zbekcha “tagida” yoki “ostida” deymiz. Masalan, “The shoes are under the bed” — poyabzallar karavot tagida. Urg‘u birinchi bo‘g‘inda: “UN-der”.

Yana ikki predlog. “Behind” — orqasida: behind the sofa, behind the door. “In front of” — oldida: in front of the TV, in front of the house. Diqqat qiling: “in front of” uch so‘zdan iborat, va oxirida “of” bor. O‘zbek tilida “orqasida” va “oldida” bitta so‘z, ingliz tilida esa birinchisi bitta, ikkinchisi uchta. “Behind” ning ham, “in front of” ning ham qarama-qarshisi bor: behind — in front of.

Uchta tipik xato. Birinchisi: “behind of”. O‘zbekchada ko‘pincha “ning orqasida” deymiz, shu sabab “of” qo‘shib yuboramiz. Ingliz tilida “behind the sofa”, “of” kerak emas. Ikkinchisi teskarisi: “in front the house”, bu yerda “of” yetishmaydi. To‘g‘risi “in front of”. Uchinchisi: “next of”, aslida “next to”.

To‘rtta predlog yana. “Next to” — yonida, tegib turgan holda: next to the bank. “Near” — yaqinida, biroz masofada: near the park. “Between” — orasida, va u doim ikkita narsani talab qiladi: between the bank and the shop. “Opposite” — qarshisida, yo‘lning narigi tomonida: opposite the school. Tartib yana teskari: predlog oldin.

Bu yerda xato odatda “between” ga bitta narsa qo‘yishdan chiqadi. “Orasida” degan so‘z o‘zbekchada bitta ot bilan ham bo‘lishi mumkin, masalan, “stullar orasida”. Ingliz tilida esa “between” ikkita aniq narsani talab qiladi, ular “and” bilan bog‘lanadi: between the table and the chair. Yoki “between the two chairs”. “Of” bu yerda ham ortiqcha.

Rasmni tasavvur qiling. Stol ustida chiroq turibdi. Stol tagida mushuk yotibdi. Sumka divan orqasida. To‘p esa divan bilan stul orasida. Endi uchta savolga javob bering. Birinchi: where is the lamp? Ikkinchi: where is the cat? Uchinchi: where is the ball? To‘liq gap bilan ayting.

Javoblar. Chiroq stol ustida: “The lamp is on the table”. Mushuk stol tagida: “The cat is under the table”. To‘p divan va stul orasida: “The ball is between the sofa and the chair”. Gap tuzilishi bir xil: ega, “is”, predlog, “the” va joy nomi. Shuni yodlab olsangiz, istalgan narsaning joyini ayta olasiz.

Narsa qayerdaligini so‘rash uchun “Where is” yoki “Where are” ishlatamiz. Bitta narsa bo‘lsa “Where is the cat?”, ko‘p bo‘lsa “Where are the keys?”. Qisqa shakl: “Where’s my phone?”. Javobda olmosh: “It’s on the bed” yoki “They’re in the bag”. O‘tgan darsda o‘rganganingizdek, bitta narsa uchun “it”, ko‘plik uchun “they”. Savol tartibi: Where, keyin is yoki are, keyin ega.

Savolda ikki xil xato bor. Birinchisi: ko‘plikdagi narsa uchun “is” ishlatish: “Where is my keys?”. Ko‘p narsa bo‘lsa “Where are my keys?”. Ikkinchisi: so‘z tartibi: “Where the cat is?”. Ingliz tilida savolda fe’l ega oldida keladi: “Where is the cat?”. Va javob berayotganda narsaga “he” yoki “she” demang, “it” deng.

Joy haqida ha yoki yo‘q javobli savollar ham beramiz. Tuzilishi: Is yoki Are, ega, predlog. “Is the cat under the table?” Javob: “Yes, it is” yoki “No, it isn’t”. Ko‘plik uchun: “Are the keys in the bag?” “Yes, they are” yoki “No, they aren’t”. Yo‘q desangiz, ko‘pincha to‘g‘ri joyni ham aytasiz: “No, it isn’t. It’s on the sofa”. Bu suhbatni tabiiy qiladi va foydali.

Yana rasm: kitob tokchada, kalitlar sumkada, mushuk stul tagida. Uchta savolga ha yoki yo‘q bilan javob bering. Birinchi: is the book on the table? Ikkinchi: are the keys in the bag? Uchinchi: is the cat on the chair? Agar javob yo‘q bo‘lsa, to‘g‘ri joyni ham ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi savol: kitob stol ustidami? “No, it isn’t. It’s on the shelf”. Ikkinchi: kalitlar sumkadami? “Yes, they are”. Uchinchi: mushuk stul ustidami? “No, it isn’t. It’s under the chair”. Yo‘q javobida “it isn’t” ni, ha javobida “they are” ni qisqartirmasdan ayting, ya’ni “Yes, they’re” demang. Qisqa javoblarda “are” oxirida to‘liq o‘qiladi.

Narsani topganda ikkita foydali ibora bor. “Here” — mana bu yerda, yaqinda. “There” — anavi yerda, uzoqroqda. “Here it is!” — mana u! “There they are!” — anavi ular! Bu iboralarda olmosh fe’ldan oldin turadi, ya’ni “Here is it” emas. Uzoqdagi joyni ko‘rsatish uchun “over there” ham aytamiz. Bu qisqa iboralar kundalik suhbatda juda ko‘p ishlatiladi.

Bitta muhim predlog yana: “at”. U aniq nuqtani yoki odam odatda shug‘ullanadigan joyni bildiradi. At home, at school, at work, at the bus stop. O‘zbekchada “uyda”, “maktabda”, “ishda” deymiz, hammasi “-da”. Ingliz tilida esa bu odatiy joylar uchun “at”. Katta joylar uchun, masalan shahar yoki mamlakat, “in”: in London, in Uzbekistan. Xonalar uchun ham “in”: in the kitchen.

Mana juda keng tarqalgan xato: “in home”. “Uyda” degan so‘zni so‘zma-so‘z o‘girsak, “in home” chiqadi. Lekin inglizlar “at home” deydi. Xuddi shunday “at work” va “at school”. Maktab uchun ham odatiy shakl “at school”. Qoida: odam odatdagi joyda bo‘lsa, at home, at work, at school. Binoning ichidagi xona uchun esa “in the kitchen”, “in the bedroom”.

Rasmni tasvirlashda yana to‘rtta ibora kerak. “On the left” — chapda, “on the right” — o‘ngda. “In the middle” — o‘rtada. “In the corner” — burchakda. Rasmning tepasi “at the top”, pasti “at the bottom”. Diqqat qiling: chap va o‘ng uchun “on”, o‘rta va burchak uchun “in”.

Chap va o‘ng haqida ham xato bo‘ladi. O‘zbekcha “chapda”, “o‘ngda” deganda “-da” bor, shu sababli ko‘pchilik “in the left” deydi. Ingliz tilida esa “on the left”, “on the right”. “In the middle” va “in the corner” da esa “in” qoladi. Rasm haqida gapirsangiz, “in the picture” deymiz, devordagi rasmni esa “on the wall” deymiz. Bu uchta farqni eslab qoling.

Rasmni tasvirlash uchun uch qadam bor. Birinchi: rasm nima haqida: “It’s a living room”. Ikkinchi: asosiy narsalar, “there is” va “there are” bilan: “In the picture there is a sofa and two chairs”. Uchinchi: har bir narsaning joyi: “On the left there is a lamp”, “On the right there are two plants”. Tartib: umumiy, keyin tafsilot.

Yotoqxona rasmini tasvirlang. Karavot o‘rtada. Karavot yonidagi stol ustida chiroq va telefon bor. Karavot tagida sumka. Devorda rasm osilgan. Hech bo‘lmasa to‘rtta gap ayting: “It’s a bedroom. There is a bed in the middle…”. Joy predloglarini ishlating va ko‘plikka e’tibor bering.

Namuna. “It’s a bedroom. There is a bed in the middle. There is a table next to the bed. On the table there is a lamp and a phone. There is a bag under the bed. There is a picture on the wall.” Qarang, hammasi bir xil tuzilmada: there is, narsa, predlog, joy. Sizniki farq qilishi mumkin, muhimi is va are, ko‘plik va joy predlogi to‘g‘ri bo‘lsin.

Oldingi darsdagi olmoshlarni bugungi predloglar bilan birlashtiramiz. Predlogdan keyin tushum olmoshi keladi: next to him, behind her, in front of us, with them, under it. Masalan, “Sit next to me” — yonimga o‘tiring. “Next to I” yoki “behind he” deyish xato. Predlogdan keyin faqat me, you, him, her, it, us, them.

Uchta ibora bor. Predlogdan keyin qavs ichidagi olmoshning to‘g‘ri shaklini qo‘ying. Birinchi: next to, men. Ikkinchi: behind, u ayol. Uchinchi: in front of, ular. Har birini baland ovozda ayting va gap ichida ishlating, masalan, “The cat is next to…”.

Javoblar. Next to me, chunki predlogdan keyin “I” emas, “me”. Behind her, ayolga nisbatan “her”. In front of them, ular uchun “them”. Agar to‘g‘ri aytgan bo‘lsangiz, oldingi darsdagi tushum olmoshlarini yaxshi o‘zlashtirgansiz. Shuni yodda tuting: har safar predlogdan keyin “I”, “he”, “she”, “we”, “they” kelmaydi.

Predloglar odatda kuchsiz aytiladi, chunki muhim ma’noni narsa nomi tashiydi. “In front of” da “of” juda yengil, “əv” yoki hatto “ə”. “Next to” da ikki “t” yonma-yon, shuning uchun “next” dagi “t” aytilmaydi: “neks tə”. “Between” va “behind” da urg‘u ikkinchi bo‘g‘inda: be-TWEEN, be-HIND. “Under” va “opposite” da birinchi bo‘g‘inda: UN-der, OP-o-zit.

Endi takrorlang. In front of the house, bu yerda “of” yengil. Next to the bank, “t” tushib qoladi. Between the shop and the bank, urg‘u TWEEN da. Behind the sofa, urg‘u HIND da. Har bir ibora uchun men aytganimdan keyin takrorlang. Predloglarni yengil, otlarni aniq o‘qing.

Birinchi dialogni tinglang. O‘g‘il onasidan kalitlari qayerdaligini so‘raydi. Ona avval stol ustida, chiroq yonida deydi, lekin kalitlar u yerda emas. Keyin ona sumkaga qarashni maslahat beradi. Diqqat qiling: on, next to, in, under. Savollar: ona avval kalitlar qayerda deb o‘ylaydi? Ular aslida qayerda va nima tagida?

Dialog bo‘yicha uchta savol. Birinchi: ona avval kalitlar qayerda deb o‘ylaydi? Ikkinchi: u qayerga qarashni aytadi? Uchinchi: kalitlar aslida qayerda va nima tagida? Predloglardan foydalanib, to‘liq gap bilan javob bering. Masalan, “They’re …”. Keyin to‘g‘ri javobni eshitasiz.

Javoblar. Ona kalitlar stol ustida, chiroq yonida deb o‘ylaydi: “on the table, next to the lamp”. U sumkaga qarashni aytadi: “in your bag”. Kalitlar sumkada va kitob tagida: “in my bag, under my book”. “Your bag” va “my book” — o‘tgan darsdagi egalik sifatlari.

Ikkinchi dialog. Sayyoh yo‘lovchidan bank qayerdaligini so‘raydi. U muloyim boshlaydi: “Excuse me”. Yo‘lovchi bankning joyini ikki xil tasvirlaydi. Diqqat qiling: next to, opposite, near, between. Savollar: bank nimaning yonida? U nimaning qarshisida? U yaqin joydami? Va kafe bilan dorixona orasida nima bor?

To‘rtta savol. Birinchi: bank nimaning yonida? Ikkinchi: bank nimaning qarshisida? Uchinchi: u yaqin joydami? To‘rtinchi: kafe va dorixona orasida nima bor? Qisqa va aniq javob bering. Dialogdagi “it” bank haqida ekanini unutmang. Javoblarni birozdan keyin tekshiramiz.

Javoblar. Bank pochtaning yonida: “the post office”. Bank bog‘ qarshisida: “the park”. U yaqinmi? “Yes, it is”. Kafe va dorixona orasida bank joylashgan: “the bank”. Ko‘rdingizmi, bitta bank uchun to‘rtta predlog ishlatildi. Yo‘l so‘rashda ham aynan shu iboralar kerak bo‘ladi.

Mana oxirgi grammatik mashq. To‘rtta o‘zbekcha gapni inglizchaga o‘giring. Kitob stol ustida. Sumka karavot tagida. Mushuk divan orqasida. Men uydaman. Tartibni eslang: ega, is, predlog, the, ot. Va oxirgi gapda “at” ni o‘ylab ko‘ring. Baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. “The book is on the table”, “The bag is under the bed”, “The cat is behind the sofa”, “I am at home”. Hammasida o‘zbekcha tartib teskari aylandi: ot oldin, joy so‘zi keyin emas, balki ega, is, predlog va keyin ot. Va “uyda” uchun “at home”.

Endi o‘zingizning xonangizni tasvirlang. Kamida to‘rtta gap ayting. Karavot, stol, deraza, kitob, kompyuter yoki boshqa narsalar haqida. “There is a bed next to the window”, “My books are on the shelf”. Turli predloglarni ishlating: in, on, under, next to yoki between. Rasmni tasavvur qiling va ravon ayting.

Namuna. “There is a bed next to the window. There is a desk in the corner. My books are on the shelf. My bag is under the desk. The laptop is on the desk, between the lamp and the cup.” Siz bu yerda there is, my va predloglardan bir vaqtning o‘zida foydalandingiz.

Xulosa. Birinchi: ingliz tilida joy so‘zi oldin, ot keyin: on the table. In ichida, on ustida, under tagida, behind orqasida, in front of oldida. Ikkinchi: next to, near, opposite, va between ikkita narsa orasida. Uchinchi: at home, at work, at school. To‘rtinchi: where is yoki where are bilan so‘raymiz, it yoki they bilan javob beramiz. Beshinchi: rasmni there is va there are bilan tasvirlaymiz.

Uyga vazifa: xonangizdagi sakkizta narsaning joyini ingliz tilida yozing va baland ovozda ayting. Mezon: sakkizta gap, kamida beshta turli predlog, kamida bitta “between … and …”, bitta “there are” va bitta “Where is …?” savoli bo‘lsin. “In home” kabi xato bo‘lmasin. Keyingi darsda transport va chipta olishni o‘rganamiz.
