Ingliz tili. 42-dars. Miqdor so‘zlari: ko‘p, oz, biroz

Assalomu alaykum! Oldingi darsda sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan otlarni, some va any ni o‘rgandik. Bugun shu bilimga tayanib miqdor so‘zlarini o‘rganamiz: ko‘p, oz, biroz, ortiqcha va yetarli. Dars oxirida o‘zingizning ovqatlanishingiz haqida to‘g‘ri miqdor so‘zlari bilan gapira olasiz.

O‘zbek tilida “ko‘p” so‘zi hamma narsaga mos: ko‘p kitob, ko‘p suv. Inglizchada esa ikkita so‘z bor. Sanaladigan ko‘plik ot bilan many ishlatiladi: many books, many people. Sanalmaydigan ot bilan much: much water, much time, much money. Tanlash uchun otga qarang: bittalab sanasa bo‘ladimi? Bo‘lsa many, bo‘lmasa much.

Muhim narsa: much va many asosan inkor gap va savolda ishlatiladi. “I don’t have much time”, “Do you have many friends?” Darak gapda esa ular rasmiy, kitobiy eshitiladi va oddiy nutqda deyarli aytilmaydi. Bunday gapda biz boshqa ibora ishlatamiz, uni hozir ko‘ramiz.

Darak gapda ko‘p degan ma’noni “a lot of” beradi. Uning yaxshi tomoni: u sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan ham, sanalmaydigan ot bilan ham ishlaydi. “A lot of friends”, “a lot of tea”. Yana “lots of” bor, u biroz erkinroq. Esda tuting: of ni tushirib qoldirmang. “A lot friends” xato, lekin “a lot” o‘zi alohida, gap oxirida kelishi mumkin: “I like it a lot”.

Bitta “ko‘p” so‘zi uchun inglizchada uchta tanlov borligi dastlab chalkashtiradi. Oddiy yo‘l: avval gap turiga qarang. Darak gap bo‘lsa, a lot of ni ishlating, u hamma narsaga mos. Inkor yoki savol bo‘lsa, otni tekshiring: sanaladigan bo‘lsa many, sanalmaydigan bo‘lsa much. Shu ikki qadam sizni xatolardan saqlaydi.

Ikki mashhur xato. Birinchisi: sanalmaydigan ot bilan many, masalan “many money”. Pul sanalmaydi, to‘g‘risi “much money” yoki darak gapda “a lot of money”. Ikkinchisi: sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan much, masalan “much friends”. To‘g‘risi “many friends” yoki darak gapda “a lot of friends”. Xatoni qanday topish kerak: ot turiga qarang.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: I don’t have blank time. Ikkinchi: How blank students are there? Uchinchi: darak gap, we have blank homework today. To‘rtinchi: Do you eat blank vegetables? Much, many yoki a lot of ni tanlang va baland ovozda ayting. Shoshilmang: avval gap turini aniqlang, keyin otning turini. Javobni baland ovozda, to‘liq gap qilib ayting.

Birinchi: “I don’t have much time”, vaqt sanalmaydi. Ikkinchi: “How many students are there?”, talabalar sanaladi. Uchinchi: darak gap, “We have a lot of homework today”; homework sanalmaydi, lekin a lot of ikkalasiga ham mos. To‘rtinchi: “Do you eat many vegetables?”, vegetables ko‘plikda sanaladi. Hammasi to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ofarin.

Endi oz miqdor. “A few” — bir nechta, sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan: a few apples, a few minutes. “A little” — biroz, sanalmaydigan ot bilan: a little milk, a little time. Mantiq many va much bilan bir xil: sanaladigan bo‘lsa few, sanalmaydigan bo‘lsa little. Ikkalasi ham ijobiy ma’noda: ozgina bor, bu yetarli.

Diqqat: a ni tashlab yuborsak, ma’no o‘zgaradi. “I have a few friends” — bir nechta do‘stim bor, yaxshi. “I have few friends” — deyarli do‘stim yo‘q, afsus. Little ham shunday: “a little time” — biroz vaqt bor, “little time” — vaqt deyarli yo‘q. Hozircha ikkinchi shaklni tanib olsangiz yetadi. Gapirganda esa a few va a little ni ishlating.

Eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato: few va little ni aralashtirish. “A few water” deb bo‘lmaydi, water sanalmaydi, to‘g‘risi “a little water”. “A little apples” ham xato: to‘g‘risi “a few apples”. Yodlash uchun oddiy hiyla: few va friends — ikkalasi ham f harfi bilan boshlanadi, sanaladigan ot bilan keladi. Little esa suyuqlik kabi massa bilan.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: we need blank sugar. Ikkinchi: I have blank questions. Uchinchi: there is blank cheese. To‘rtinchi: she bought blank oranges. A few yoki a little ni tanlang va gapni to‘liq ayting. Har bir otni o‘zingizga savol bilan tekshiring: uni bittalab sanasa bo‘ladimi? Keyin few yoki little ni tanlab, gapni to‘liq ayting.

Javoblar: “We need a little sugar”, shakar sanalmaydi. “I have a few questions”, savollar sanaladi. “There is a little cheese”, pishloq sanalmaydi. “She bought a few oranges”, apelsinlar sanaladi. Agar ikkitasini adashtirgan bo‘lsangiz, yuqoridagi hiylani eslang: few — sanaladigan, little — massa.

Hammasini bir joyga yig‘amiz. Faqat sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan: many, a few, few. Faqat sanalmaydigan ot bilan: much, a little, little. Ikkalasi bilan ishlaydiganlar: a lot of, lots of, some, any, enough. Shu uch qatorni yodlab olsangiz, tanlashda doim to‘g‘ri yo‘l topasiz. Yodlash uchun ularni muzlatgich eshigiga yozib qo‘ying.

Miqdorni so‘rash uchun How many va How much bor. Sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan How many: how many eggs, how many brothers. Sanalmaydigan ot bilan How much: how much milk, how much time. So‘ng yordamchi fe’l: “How much sugar do you take?” Javob ko‘pincha son yoki o‘lchov iborasi: “Two”, “A little”, “Half a kilo”.

How much yana bir muhim vazifani bajaradi: narxni so‘raydi. Birlikda: “How much is this bread?” Ko‘plikda fe’l o‘zgaradi: “How much are these apples?” Bu yerda much narxga ishora qiladi, shuning uchun ko‘plik ot bo‘lsa ham fe’l are bo‘ladi. Javob: “It’s two pounds” yoki “They’re three pounds”. Do‘konda eng kerakli savol.

Ikkita xato. Birinchisi: “How many money”. Pul sanalmaydi, to‘g‘risi “How much money”. Ikkinchisi: “How much people”. People sanaladi, to‘g‘risi “How many people”. Yana bir tuzoq: how many dan keyin fe’l ko‘plikka mos: “How many people are there?”, “is there” emas. Savol tuzayotganda avval otni aniqlang.

Bo‘sh joyga how much yoki how many ni qo‘ying. Birinchi: blank sugar do you take? Ikkinchi: blank children does she have? Uchinchi: blank is this ticket? To‘rtinchi: blank cars are there? Savolni to‘liq ayting. Savolning kaliti ot turi va narx ma’nosi. Narx bo‘lsa how much, sanaladigan ko‘plik bo‘lsa how many. Tayyor bo‘lsangiz, savolni baland ovozda ayting.

Birinchi: “How much sugar do you take?”, shakar sanalmaydi. Ikkinchi: “How many children does she have?”, bolalar sanaladi. Uchinchi: “How much is this ticket?”, bu yerda narx so‘ralyapti. To‘rtinchi: “How many cars are there?”, mashinalar sanaladi. Narx so‘rasangiz doim how much.

Too much va too many — “ortiqcha ko‘p”, ya’ni me’yordan oshgan va bu yomon. Tuzilishi oddiy: too much + sanalmaydigan ot, too many + sanaladigan ko‘plik ot. “Too much sugar”, “too many cars”. Bu iboralar darak gapda ham bemalol ishlatiladi, chunki ular ijobiy ma’noda emas, shikoyat ohangida. Masalan: “I eat too much bread”.

Too sifat bilan ham ishlatiladi: “too hot”, “too big”, “too late”. Bu yerda much va many kerak emas: “too much hot” xato. Qoida: too + sifat; too much/many + ot. “The soup is too hot” — sho‘rva juda issiq, ichib bo‘lmaydi. “There is too much soup” — sho‘rva juda ko‘p. Ikkala gapning mantiqi har xil, fikr yuritib tanlang.

Enough — yetarli. Otdan oldin keladi: “enough time”, “enough chairs”. Sifatdan keyin keladi: “big enough”, “old enough”. Bu tartibni yodlab oling, chunki o‘zbekchada “yetarlicha katta” desak, so‘z tartibi boshqacha. Inkor shakl: “not enough”: “There isn’t enough milk”. Enough sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan ham, sanalmaydigan ot bilan ham ishlaydi.

Ikki xato. Birinchisi: enough ni sifatdan oldin qo‘yish, “enough big”. To‘g‘risi “big enough”. Ikkinchisi: sanaladigan ot bilan too much, “too much people”. To‘g‘risi “too many people”. Qoida: sifatdan keyin enough; sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan too many; sanalmaydigan ot bilan too much.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: ko‘chada mashina juda ko‘p, there are blank cars. Ikkinchi: shovqin juda ko‘p, there is blank noise. Uchinchi: sumka noutbuk uchun yetarlicha katta emas. To‘rtinchi: bizda vaqt yetarli. Inglizchasini ayting. Har bir gapda ma’noga qarang: me’yordan ortiqmi yoki yetarlimi. Ortiq bo‘lsa too, yetarli bo‘lsa enough. Enough ning o‘rniga ham e’tibor bering.

Birinchi: “There are too many cars in the street”, mashinalar sanaladi. Ikkinchi: “There is too much noise”, shovqin sanalmaydi. Uchinchi: “The bag is not big enough for my laptop”, enough sifatdan keyin. To‘rtinchi: “We have enough time. Let’s go!”, enough otdan oldin. Hammasini topdingizmi? Zo‘r.

Birinchi dialog shifokor qabulida. Shifokor bemorning odatlari haqida so‘raydi. How much va how many savollariga, hamda bemor qanday qisqa javob berishiga e’tibor bering. Keyin savollarga javob berasiz. Shifokor suv va qahva miqdorini so‘rayapti. Raqamlarni va shifokorning xulosasini eslab qoling.

Uchta savol. Birinchi: bemor qancha suv ichadi? Ikkinchi: u nechta qahva ichadi? Uchinchi: shifokor nima deb o‘ylaydi? Javobni to‘liq gap bilan ayting, miqdor so‘zini ishlating. Javobda dialogdagi so‘zlardan foydalaning. Oxirgi savolda shifokorning fikrini o‘z so‘zingiz bilan ham aytsangiz bo‘ladi.

Birinchi: u ko‘p suv ichmaydi, “only a little”. Ikkinchi: besh-olti qahva ichadi, “five or six”. Uchinchi: shifokor “that’s too many” deydi, ya’ni qahva juda ko‘p. Ko‘rdingizmi, qisqa javobda ham miqdor so‘zi tabiiy ishlatiladi: “a little”, “too many”.

Ikkinchi dialog: ikki do‘st ziyofatga tayyorlanmoqda. Ular odamlar soni, stullar va ovqat haqida gaplashadi. Enough, a few va how many qanday ishlatilganiga quloq soling, keyin ikkita savol bo‘ladi. Bu yerda odamlar soni, stullar va sharbat haqida gap ketadi. Raqamlarni yodda tuting, chunki savollar ularga tegishli.

Savollar. Birinchi: nechta mehmon keladi? Ikkinchi: nima uchun ularga ko‘proq stul kerak? Uchinchi: Lili nima sotib oladi? Javobni inglizcha, to‘liq gap bilan ayting. Ikkinchi savolda sababni ham ayting, ya’ni stullar yetarli emasligini. Uchinchi savolda esa o‘lchov iborasi kerak bo‘ladi.

Birinchi: taxminan o‘n ikkita mehmon. Ikkinchi: ularda faqat sakkizta stul bor, bu yetarli emas, “not enough”, shuning uchun yana bir nechta, “a few more”, kerak. Uchinchi: Lili ikki litr sharbat sotib oladi, “two litres of juice”, chunki sharbat ko‘p emas, “there isn’t much juice”. O‘tgan darsdagi o‘lchov iborasini ham ko‘rdingiz.

Gapirganda ko‘pincha to‘liq gap aytilmaydi. How many ga javob: “A lot”, “Quite a few” — birmuncha, “Not many” — ko‘p emas, “None” — hech qancha. How much ga: “A lot”, “A little”, “Not much”, “None”. Shu qisqa javoblar suhbatni tabiiy qiladi. Esda tuting: sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan farqi javobda ham saqlanadi: “Not many”, lekin “Not much”.

Ingliz tilida yana bir necha tabiiy ibora bor. “A bit of” — biroz, “a little” ning erkin varianti: “a bit of help”, “a bit of sugar”. “Plenty of” — yetarlidan ham ko‘p: “We have plenty of time”, vaqtimiz mo‘l. Va juda erkin “loads of” — Britaniyada keng tarqalgan, “loads of people”. Rasmiy yozuvda esa a lot of va a large number of ishlatiladi.

Uchta savolga qisqa va tabiiy javob bering. Birinchi: nechta til bilasiz? Ikkinchi: choyga qancha shakar solasiz? Uchinchi: kuniga nechta piyola choy ichasiz? Javobda a few, a little, a lot, not many yoki not much ni ishlating. Savollar kundalik, shuning uchun javoblar qisqa bo‘lsin. To‘liq gap shart emas, lekin sanaladigan va sanalmaydigan farqi saqlansin: not many yoki not much.

Mana variantlar. Birinchi: “A few” yoki aniq son, “Three”. Ikkinchi: “Just a little” yoki “Not much”. Uchinchi: “Quite a lot” yoki “Not many”. Sizniki boshqacha bo‘lishi mumkin, muhimi tanlov to‘g‘ri bo‘lsin: languages va cups uchun many yoki a few, sugar uchun much yoki a little. Ofarin!

Oldin aytdik, darak gapda much kam ishlatiladi. Lekin bir necha tayyor iboralar bor: “very much”, “too much”, “so much”, “as much as”. “Thank you very much” — katta rahmat. “I miss you so much” — sizni juda sog‘indim. “Eat as much as you like” — xohlaganingizcha yeng. Ularni yodlab oling, bular qoidadan istisno emas, shunchaki qotib qolgan iboralar.

Tabiiy nutqda “a lot of” bir so‘zdek aytiladi, a, lot, of tez qo‘shilib, “a lotta” kabi. Urg‘u lot ga tushadi. Much — “mach”, qisqa unli va ch bilan tugaydi. Many — “meni”, birinchi bo‘g‘inda urg‘u, e unlisi “e” kabi, “mani” emas. Few — “fyu”, little — “litl”, ikkinchi l bo‘g‘in hosil qiladi.

Meni takrorlang. A lot of ni tez va qo‘shib ayting, many da birinchi bo‘g‘inga urg‘u bering, much da unli qisqa bo‘lsin. Har bir iborani ikki marta aytib chiqing. Bu talaffuz mashqi. Tezlikni oshirmang, avval aniqlik. Ma’noli so‘zlarni kuchli, of ni esa yengil ayting va har bir iborani ikki marta takrorlang.

Yaxshi chiqdi. Esda tuting: ingliz tilida so‘zlar ajratib emas, ketma-ket oqim bilan aytiladi. “A lot of people” — birdan, to‘xtashsiz. Bu odatni o‘zlashtirsangiz, nutqingiz ancha tabiiy eshitiladi. Keyingi bosqichda o‘zingiz haqingizda gapirasiz, endi bilimlarni birlashtiramiz.

Endi o‘zingiz gapiring. Ovqatlanishingiz haqida to‘rtta gap ayting. Masalan: I eat a lot of bread. I don’t drink much milk. I eat a few apples every day. I drink too much tea. Kamida to‘rtta xil miqdor so‘zi ishlating. Gapingizni sekin tuzing, shoshilmang. Har bir gapda boshqa miqdor so‘zi bo‘lsin va ot turiga mos bo‘lsin. Xato qilishdan qo‘rqmang, bu mashq uchun.

Mana boshqa namuna. “I eat a lot of rice and a few vegetables. I don’t drink much water. I eat too much sugar and I don’t eat enough fruit.” Ko‘rdingizmi, darak gapda a lot of, inkorda much, sanaladigan sabzavot bilan a few, shikoyat bilan too much, yetishmovchilik uchun not enough. Siz ham shunday birlashtirdingizmi?

Yakuniy mashq: to‘rtta gapdagi xatoni to‘g‘rilang. Birinchi: I don’t have many time. Ikkinchi: there are too much cars. Uchinchi: how many money do you need? To‘rtinchi: we have a little eggs. Har birini to‘g‘ri ayting. Har bir gapda xato bor. Avval otning turini aniqlang, keyin miqdor so‘zini almashtiring. Javobingizni to‘liq gap bilan ayting.

To‘g‘ri variantlar. Birinchi: “I don’t have much time”, vaqt sanalmaydi. Ikkinchi: “There are too many cars”, mashinalar sanaladi. Uchinchi: “How much money do you need?”, pul sanalmaydi. To‘rtinchi: “We have a few eggs”, tuxumlar sanaladi. Har bir xatoning ildizi bitta edi: ot turini tekshirmaslik.

Xulosa qilamiz. Sanaladigan ko‘plik ot bilan many, a few, too many. Sanalmaydigan ot bilan much, a little, too much. Ikkalasi bilan a lot of, enough, some, any. Darak gapda a lot of, inkor va savolda much va many. Savolda sanaladigan ot bilan how many, sanalmaydigan ot yoki narx bilan how much. Sifatdan keyin enough.

Uy vazifasi: ovqatlanishingizni sakkizta miqdor so‘zi bilan tasvirlang. Masalan, a lot of, much, many, a few, a little, too much, too many, enough. Tekshiring: many faqat sanaladigan ko‘plik bilan, much faqat sanalmaydigan ot bilan, darak gapda a lot of. Keyingi darsda va, lekin, chunki, shuning uchun kabi bog‘lovchilarni o‘rganamiz.
