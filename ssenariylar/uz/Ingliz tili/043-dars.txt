Ingliz tili. 43-dars. Bog‘lovchilar: va, lekin, chunki, shuning uchun

Assalomu alaykum! Oldingi darsda ko‘p, oz, biroz kabi miqdor so‘zlarini o‘rgandik. Bugun gaplarni bir-biriga bog‘laymiz. Hozirgacha siz qisqa gaplar bilan gapirdingiz, bugundan boshlab esa fikringizni bog‘lab, sabab va natijani aytib, uzunroq va chiroyliroq gapira olasiz. Dars oxirida sakkiz juft gapni birlashtira olasiz.

Bog‘lovchi — ikki fikrni bog‘laydigan so‘z. Bog‘lovchisiz gaplar qisqa va uzuq-yuluq eshitiladi: “I am tired. I go to bed.” Bog‘lovchi bilan esa fikr o‘rtasidagi aloqa ko‘rinadi: “I am tired, so I go to bed.” Bugun to‘rtta eng muhim bog‘lovchini o‘rganamiz: and, but, because va so. Or ni ham qisqacha ko‘ramiz.

And — “va”. U ikkita so‘zni, iborani yoki gapni qo‘shadi. “Tea and coffee”, “cold and wet”. Ikki gapni bog‘lasa ham bo‘ladi: “She opened the door and went in.” Bu yerda ikkinchi gapda ega takrorlanmaydi, chunki ega bir xil. Uch va undan ko‘p narsa sanalganda faqat oxirgisi oldidan and: “apples, oranges and bananas”.

But — “lekin, ammo”. U ikki o‘zaro qarama-qarshi fikrni bog‘laydi: “The film was long, but it was good.” Bu yerda but dan oldin vergul qo‘yamiz, agar ikki to‘liq gap bog‘lanayotgan bo‘lsa. Ikki so‘zni bog‘lasa vergul shart emas: “small but comfortable”. O‘zbekcha “lekin”, “ammo”, “biroq” ning hammasi inglizchada but ga to‘g‘ri keladi.

Or — “yoki”. U tanlovni bildiradi. “Tea or coffee?” Savolda or odatda ikkinchi variantga tushadigan ohangda aytiladi. Inkor gapda ham or ishlatiladi, and emas: “I don’t like tea or coffee” — men na choyni, na qahvani yoqtiraman. Bu o‘zbekchadagi “va” bilan farqli, shuning uchun e’tibor bering.

Ikki xato. Birinchisi: inkor gapda and ishlatish. “I don’t like tea and coffee” deganda ma’no “ikkalasini birga yoqtirmayman” bo‘lib qoladi. To‘g‘risi “tea or coffee”. Ikkinchisi: qarama-qarshi fikrda and ishlatish. Agar fikrlar qarama-qarshi bo‘lsa, and emas, but kerak: “I like tea, but I don’t like coffee”.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: I like football blank I don’t like tennis. Ikkinchi: Do you want water blank juice? Uchinchi: She is tall blank thin. To‘rtinchi: It’s sunny blank cold. And, but yoki or ni tanlang va gapni baland ovozda ayting.

Birinchi: “I like football, but I don’t like tennis”, qarama-qarshilik. Ikkinchi: “Do you want water or juice?”, tanlov. Uchinchi: “She is tall and thin”, bir yo‘nalishdagi ikkita xususiyat. To‘rtinchi: “It’s sunny, but cold”, quyoshli, lekin sovuq: kutilmagan farq. Agar adashgan bo‘lsangiz, o‘zingizdan so‘rang: fikrlar bir tomonlama yoki qarama-qarshimi?

Because — “chunki”, u sababni kiritadi. Tartib: avval natija yoki holat, keyin because, keyin sabab. “I stayed at home because I was ill.” Muhim: because dan keyin to‘liq gap keladi, ya’ni ega va fe’l bor: “because I was ill”. Oldidan vergul odatda qo‘yilmaydi. Bir savolga javob sifatida because ni qisqa aytsa ham bo‘ladi: “Why? Because I was tired.”

So — “shuning uchun”, u natijani kiritadi. Tartib because ning teskarisi: avval sabab, keyin so, keyin natija. “I was ill, so I stayed at home.” So oldidan vergul qo‘yamiz, chunki ikki to‘liq gap bog‘lanyapti. Ikkala gap ham bir xil fikrni aytadi, faqat tartib boshqa: “I stayed at home because I was ill” va “I was ill, so I stayed at home”.

Bir fikrni ikki yo‘l bilan aytish mumkin. Because bilan: natija, because, sabab. So bilan: sabab, vergul, so, natija. “He is tired because he worked late.” Yoki “He worked late, so he is tired.” Qaysi birini tanlash — nimaga e’tibor qaratayotganingizga bog‘liq. Savol “Nega?” bo‘lsa, odatda because bilan javob berasiz.

O‘zbek tilida sabab ko‘pincha “-gani uchun” qo‘shimchasi bilan beriladi: “Kasal bo‘lgani uchun uyda qoldim.” Fe’lning shakli o‘zgaradi. Inglizchada esa shunday qo‘shimcha yo‘q, buning o‘rniga because bilan alohida to‘liq gap tuziladi: “because I was ill”, ya’ni ega, fe’l va vaqt shakli saqlanadi. Shuning uchun inglizcha gap odatda o‘zbekchadan uzunroq bo‘ladi, bu normal.

Eng katta xato: sabab va natijani aralashtirish. “I was ill, because I stayed at home” — bu degani “uyda qolganim uchun kasal bo‘ldim”, ma’no teskari. Qoida oddiy: because dan keyin sabab keladi, so dan keyin natija keladi. Gap yozishdan oldin o‘zingizga ayting: qaysi biri sabab, qaysi biri natija?

Yana ikkita xato. Birinchisi: because dan keyin faqat sifat yoki ot qo‘yish, “because ill”. Because dan keyin ega va fe’l bo‘lishi shart: “because I was ill”. Ikkinchisi: because li qismni alohida gap qilib yozish: “Because I was ill.” Bu to‘liq gap emas. Uni asosiy gap bilan bitta gapda qo‘shing. Faqat savolga javob sifatida yolg‘iz aytish mumkin.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: I was thirsty blank I drank some water. Ikkinchi: I drank some water blank I was thirsty. Uchinchi: she studies hard blank she wants to pass. To‘rtinchi: it was late blank we went home. Because yoki so ni ayting.

Birinchi: “I was thirsty, so I drank some water”, chanqash sabab, ichish natija. Ikkinchi: “I drank some water because I was thirsty”, bu yerda sabab keyin keladi. Uchinchi: “She studies hard because she wants to pass”, o‘tishni xohlashi sabab. To‘rtinchi: “It was late, so we went home”, kech bo‘lishi sabab, ketish natija. Hammasini topdingizmi? Barakalla.

Vergul haqida qoida. Ikkita to‘liq gapni and, but, or yoki so bilan bog‘laganda, bog‘lovchidan oldin vergul qo‘yiladi: “I was tired, but I finished the work.” Because oldidan esa odatda vergul qo‘yilmaydi: “I stayed at home because I was ill.” Agar ikkinchi qismda ega yo‘q bo‘lsa, vergul ham kerak emas: “She opened the door and went in.”

Because li qismni gap boshiga qo‘yish ham mumkin. Unda sabab qismidan keyin vergul qo‘yiladi: “Because it was late, we went home.” Bu o‘zbekcha “kech bo‘lgani uchun, uyga ketdik” tartibiga yaqin. Yozma rasmiy matnda gapni and, but yoki so bilan boshlash tavsiya etilmaydi, suhbatda esa bu tabiiy. Maktab insholarida va xatlarda gapni to‘liq yozing.

Endi asosiy ko‘nikma: ikkita qisqa gapni bitta gapga birlashtirish. Avval ular orasidagi aloqani aniqlang: qo‘shish, qarama-qarshilik, tanlov, sabab yoki natija. Keyin mos bog‘lovchini tanlang. “He studied hard. He passed.” — bu yerda birinchisi sabab, ikkinchisi natija, demak “He studied hard, so he passed”. Yoki teskarisi: “He passed because he studied hard”.

Har bir juftni brackets ichida ko‘rsatilgan bog‘lovchi bilan bitta gapga birlashtiring. Birinchi: it was cold, I wore a coat, so. Ikkinchi: I like pizza, I don’t like burgers, but. Uchinchi: she is happy, she won, because. To‘rtinchi: I’ll call you, I’ll send a message, or.

Birinchi: “It was cold, so I wore a coat”. Ikkinchi: “I like pizza, but I don’t like burgers”. Uchinchi: “She is happy because she won”, g‘alaba — sabab. To‘rtinchi: “I’ll call you, or I’ll send a message”. Ko‘ryapsizmi, ikki to‘liq gap bo‘lganda bog‘lovchidan oldin vergul bor, because da esa yo‘q. Siz ham shunday yozdingizmi?

Bir gapda ikki bog‘lovchi ham bo‘lishi mumkin: “It was late, but I wasn’t tired, so I read a book.” Bunda har bir bog‘lovchi o‘z vazifasini bajaradi: but qarama-qarshilik, so natija. Lekin bitta sabab-natija aloqasida because va so ni birga ishlatmang. “Because I was tired, so I went home” — o‘zbek tilidan kelgan xato, chunki o‘zbekchada “chunki” va “shuning uchun” bir gapda uchrashi mumkin.

Mana juda mashhur xato: because bilan so ni bir gapda ishlatish. Ikkalasi bir xil aloqani bildiradi, shuning uchun bittasini tanlang. Yo “Because I was tired, I went home”, yo “I was tired, so I went home”. O‘zbek tilida, “chunki” va “shuning uchun” ni birga aytsak ham, inglizchada bunday qilinmaydi.

Birinchi dialogni tinglang. Ikki hamkasb ertalab uchrashadi. Biri kechikkan va sababini aytadi. Because, so va but qanday ishlatilganiga quloq soling. Keyin savollarga javob berasiz.

Uchta savol. Birinchi: Keyt nega kechikdi? Ikkinchi: u nega piyoda keldi? Uchinchi: Tom nega unga sochiq beradi? Javobni because bilan boshlang.

Birinchi: u kechikdi, chunki avtobusga ulgurmadi, “because she missed the bus”. Ikkinchi: u piyoda keldi, chunki avtobus ketib bo‘lgandi, ya’ni sabab o‘sha. Uchinchi: Tom unga sochiq beradi, chunki u sovuq qotgandek ko‘rinadi, “because she looks cold”. Dialogda Keyt “missed the bus, so I walked” dedi: bu yerda so natijani bildirdi.

Ikkinchi dialog: ikki do‘st dam olish kunini rejalashtirmoqda. Or bilan tanlov, because bilan sabab va so bilan natija qanday ishlatilganiga e’tibor bering. Savollar tinglagandan keyin.

Savollar. Birinchi: ular qayerga borishadi va nega? Ikkinchi: nega sendvich olishadi? Uchinchi: ichimliklarni kim olib keladi? Because yoki so bilan javob bering.

Birinchi: ular parkka borishadi, chunki ob-havo yaxshi, “because the weather is nice”. Ikkinchi: Ananing qorni och, shuning uchun sendvich olishadi, “she is hungry, so they take sandwiches”. Uchinchi: Ben sendvich tayyorlaydi, ichimliklarni Ana olib keladi, “you can bring the drinks”. Dialogda uchala bog‘lovchi ham ishlatildi.

Because ning yaqin qarindoshi bor: because of. Because dan keyin to‘liq gap keladi, because of dan keyin esa faqat ot yoki ot birikmasi: “because of the rain”, “because of you”. Ikkalasi ham bir ma’noni beradi. “We stayed in because it was raining” va “We stayed in because of the rain”. Bu ikkisini aralashtirmang: “because of it was raining” xato.

Tabiiy nutqda and, but, or kabi bog‘lovchilar kuchsiz aytiladi. And ko‘pincha “en” yoki shunchaki “n” bo‘lib eshitiladi: tea and coffee — “tea-n-coffee”. Because da urg‘u ikkinchi bo‘g‘inda: “bi-KOZ”. So esa kuchli, uzun “sou”, chunki natija o‘sha yerda boshlanadi. Bu kuchsiz shakllarni eshitib o‘rgansangiz, tinglab tushunish ham yaxshilanadi.

Meni takrorlang. And ni yengil, “n” kabi ayting. Because da ikkinchi bo‘g‘inni kuchli ayting. Vergulda qisqa pauza qiling. Har bir gapni ikki marta aytib chiqing.

Yaxshi. Esda tuting: yengil bog‘lovchilar ritmni yaratadi, kuchli so‘zlar esa ma’noni olib yuradi. Vergul joyida qisqa pauza qiling, chunki yozuvdagi vergul nutqda pauzaga to‘g‘ri keladi. Shunda siz uzun gapni ham tushunarli va ravon ayta olasiz.

Endi o‘zingiz gapiring. Bugungi kuningiz haqida to‘rtta gap ayting. Har birida boshqa bog‘lovchi bo‘lsin: and, but, because va so. Masalan: I got up early because I had work. I was tired, so I had coffee.

Mana namuna: “I got up early because I had work. I was tired, so I had coffee. I wanted to walk, but it was raining, so I took a bus.” Ko‘rdingizmi, sabab uchun because, natija uchun so, qarama-qarshilik uchun but. Sizning gaplaringiz boshqacha bo‘lishi mumkin. Muhimi, aloqa to‘g‘ri bo‘lsin va vergullar joyida turishi.

Yakuniy mashq: to‘rtta gapdagi xatoni toping. Birinchi: I was ill, because I stayed home. Ikkinchi: Because I was tired, so I slept. Uchinchi: I stayed home because ill. To‘rtinchi: I don’t like tea and coffee. Har birini to‘g‘rilab ayting.

Birinchi: “I was ill, so I stayed home”, sabab va natija joyi almashgan edi. Ikkinchi: “I was tired, so I slept” yoki “Because I was tired, I slept”, ikkalasini birga ishlatmang. Uchinchi: “I stayed home because I was ill”, because dan keyin to‘liq gap. To‘rtinchi: “I don’t like tea or coffee”, inkorda or. Barcha qoidalar bitta mashqda takrorlandi.

Xulosa qilamiz. And qo‘shadi, but qarama-qarshilikni bildiradi, or tanlov beradi. Because sabab, so natija. Ikki to‘liq gapni bog‘lovchi bilan bog‘laganda oldidan vergul qo‘yamiz, because dan oldin esa odatda yo‘q. Because dan keyin to‘liq gap kerak, because va so ni bir gapda birga ishlatmang. Bularni o‘zlashtirsangiz, gapingiz ancha ravon bo‘ladi.

Uy vazifasi: sakkiz juft qisqa gapni bog‘lovchilar bilan bittadan gapga birlashtiring. Kamida ikkitasida because, ikkitasida so, ikkitasida but ishlating. Tekshiring: sabab va natija adashmagan, because dan keyin to‘liq gap bor, so dan oldin vergul bor. Keyingi darsda tabiat, hayvonlar va atrof-muhit haqida gapirishni o‘rganamiz.
