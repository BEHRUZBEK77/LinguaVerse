Ingliz tili. 53-dars. O‘tmishdagi davomli harakat va hikoya

Assalomu alaykum! Oldingi darsda present perfect’ni, ya’ni tajriba va natijani o‘rgandik. Bugun o‘tmish haqida chiroyli hikoya aytishni o‘rganamiz. Buning uchun ikki zamon kerak: voqealar uchun past simple va fon uchun past continuous. Dars oxirida bir daqiqalik kutilmagan voqea haqida hikoya qila olasiz.

Past continuous — was yoki were va fe’lga -ing. I, he, she, it bilan “was”; you, we, they bilan “were”. Inkorda wasn’t yoki weren’t, savolda was yoki were gap boshiga chiqadi. O‘zbekchada “-ayotgan edi” shakliga to‘g‘ri keladi: “ovqat tayyorlayotgan edim”. Bu shakl sizga tanish, shuning uchun ingliz tilidagi mantiq oson o‘rnashadi.

Imlo qoidalari. Bir bo‘g‘inli, undosh-unli-undosh bilan tugagan fe’llarda oxirgi undosh ikkilanadi: run — running, swim — swimming. Jim -e bilan tugasa, -e tushadi: make — making, write — writing. “Lie” — “lying”. “Study” — “studying”, y qoladi. Bu qoidalar present continuous’da ham aynan shunday.

Birinchi vazifa: o‘tmishdagi aniq paytda davom etayotgan jarayon. “At eight I was having dinner” — soat sakkizda ovqatlanish jarayonining o‘rtasida edim. Qachon boshlanib, qachon tugagani noma’lum. Mana shu ma’no past simple’dan farq qiladi. Savol: “What were you doing at nine last night?” Bu so‘roq ko‘pincha detektiv hikoyalarida ham uchraydi.

Ikkinchi vazifa: fon yaratish. Hikoyani boshlaganda sahnani chizamiz: ob-havo, joy, odamlar nima qilayotgani. “It was raining and the streets were empty.” Bu past continuous. Keyin voqea boshlanganda past simple. Kinoga o‘xshatish mumkin: avval kamera manzarani ko‘rsatadi, keyin voqea sodir bo‘ladi. O‘zbekchada ham “yomg‘ir yog‘ayotgan edi” deb boshlaymiz.

To‘rtta gapni past continuous bilan to‘ldiring. Har bir gapda aniq payt bor: soat yetti, to‘qqiz, yarim tun, o‘n bir. Savol va inkorni ham to‘g‘ri tuzing. Baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. Bir — “was having”. Ikki — “were watching”, they bilan were. Uch — “were you doing”. To‘rt — “wasn’t sleeping”. Agar was va were’ni adashtirgan bo‘lsangiz, yodlab oling: faqat I, he, she, it bilan was.

Uchinchi vazifa — to‘xtatilgan harakat. Uzoq davom etayotgan jarayon past continuous’da, uni to‘xtatgan qisqa voqea past simple’da. “I was walking home when I saw an accident.” Yurish davom etayotgan edi, avariyani ko‘rish qisqa lahza. Chizma sifatida tasavvur qiling: uzun chiziq va uning ustida bitta nuqta. Chiziq — continuous, nuqta — simple.

When va while. Odatda “when” dan keyin qisqa voqea: “When the phone rang”. “While” dan keyin esa davom etayotgan jarayon: “While I was cooking”. Ikkala gapda ma’no bir xil, faqat tartib o‘zgargan. “As” ham “while” kabi ishlatiladi: “as I was leaving”. Gap boshida ishlatilsa, vergul qo‘yamiz, oxirida bo‘lsa vergul shart emas.

Ikki harakat bir vaqtda davom etsa, ikkalasi ham past continuous bo‘ladi. “While I was studying, my brother was playing music.” Ikki uzun chiziq yonma-yon turibdi. Bu hikoyada fon yaratishning yana bir usuli. Bunda hech qanday to‘xtatuvchi voqea yo‘q, shunchaki ikki parallel jarayon.

Birinchi xato: qisqa voqeani ham continuous qilish. “I was seeing an accident” — xato. Ko‘rish qisqa lahza, shuning uchun “I saw an accident”. Xuddi shunday, “arrive” qisqa voqea: “When she arrived, I was cooking”, “when she was arriving” emas. Qoida: lahzaviy voqea — past simple, davom etgan jarayon — past continuous.

Ikkinchi xato: holat fe’llarini continuous qilish. Know, want, like, love, need, understand fe’llari odatda continuous bo‘lmaydi. Shuning uchun “I knew the answer” deymiz, “I was knowing” emas. O‘zbekchada “bilib turgan edim” desak ham mumkin, inglizchada esa mumkin emas. Agar fe’l holatni ko‘rsatsa, past simple ishlating.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchisida when yoki while? Qisqa voqea alarm rang. Ikkinchisida ikki jarayon. Uchinchi va to‘rtinchisida fe’llarni to‘g‘ri zamonda yozing: qaysi biri uzoq, qaysi biri qisqa? Baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. Bir — when, chunki jiringlash qisqa voqea. Ikki — while, ikki jarayon. Uch — “saw” va “was walking”. To‘rt — “went out” va “were having”. Qisqa voqea past simple, jarayon past continuous. Shu qoidani bilsangiz, yarim yo‘lni bosib o‘tdingiz.

Endi ikki zamon o‘rtasidagi ma’no farqi. Past simple butun, tugagan voqeani bildiradi: “He painted the room” — xona to‘liq bo‘yalgan. Past continuous jarayonning o‘rtasini ko‘rsatadi: “He was painting the room” — bo‘yayotgan edi, tugatgan-tugatmagani noma’lum. Bu farq o‘zbek tilida ko‘pincha kontekstdan chiqadi, inglizchada esa zamon bilan beriladi.

Birin-ketin sodir bo‘lgan voqealar ikkalasi ham past simple’da: “When I got home, I took off my coat” — avval keldim, keyin paltoni yechdim. Lekin “When I got home, she was cooking” — kelganimda u allaqachon pishirayotgan edi. Ko‘rdingizmi, bir xil “when”, lekin zamon ma’noni o‘zgartiradi. Buni o‘zbekchada ko‘pincha faqat kontekst aytib turadi.

Past simple yoki continuous? Birinchisida oshxona tayyor. Ikkinchisida jarayon. Uchinchisida ular men kelganimdan keyin ketishgan. To‘rtinchisida men kelganimda ular allaqachon o‘ynayotgan edi. Ma’noga qarab tanlang.

Javoblar. Bir — “painted”, tugagan. Ikki — “was painting”, jarayon. Uch — “arrived” va “left”, birin-ketin. To‘rt — “arrived” va “were playing”, o‘ynash allaqachon davom etayotgan edi. Ma’noga qarab tanlash bu bobning asosiy qoidasi.

Inkor va savol shakllari oddiy. Inkorda wasn’t yoki weren’t: “I wasn’t listening”. Savolda was yoki were gap boshiga chiqadi: “Was it raining?”. Qisqa javobda “Yes, it was” yoki “No, it wasn’t”. So‘roq so‘zlari bilan: “What was she doing?”, “Where were they going?”. Bu yerda did yoki do kerak emas, chunki was va were o‘zi yordamchi fe’l.

Gaplarni o‘zgartiring. Birinchisini savolga aylantiring. Ikkinchisini inkorga. Uchinchisida “what” bilan savol tuzing. To‘rtinchisida savol bering va qisqa javob ayting. Did yoki do ishlatmang.

Javoblar. Bir — “Was she reading?”. Ikki — “They weren’t playing”. Uch — “What was he doing?”. To‘rt — “Was it snowing? Yes, it was.” Hech qaysisida did yoki do yo‘q. Shu yerda xato qilgan bo‘lsangiz, qoidani eslang: was va were o‘zi yordamchi fe’l.

Past continuous bilan keladigan vaqt belgilari: at eight o’clock, at that time, this time yesterday, all evening, at the time, at that moment. Ularning ma’nosi — o‘sha aniq paytda jarayon davom etayotgan edi. Diqqat: “yesterday” yolg‘iz bo‘lsa, past simple bilan ham kelishi mumkin, lekin “this time yesterday” jarayonni ko‘rsatadi va continuous talab qiladi.

Bir qiziq ishlatilish: always yoki constantly bilan past continuous g‘ashga tegadigan takrorlanuvchi harakatni bildiradi. “He was always losing his keys” — doim kalitini yo‘qotardi, bu menga yoqmasdi. Oddiy odat uchun esa past simple yoki “used to” ishlatiladi. Bu shaklni ko‘p ishlatmang, faqat shikoyat ohangini berish uchun.

Hikoyani to‘g‘ri zamonlarda to‘ldiring. Birinchi gapda fon: was late va was walking. Ikkinchi gapda to‘satdan voqea. Uchinchi gapda fon. To‘rtinchida voqea va tafsilot. Qaysi biri jarayon, qaysi biri voqea ekanini o‘ylang.

Javoblar. “It was late” — holat, was. “I was walking” — jarayon. “I heard” — voqea. “Someone was following me” — jarayon, fon. “I turned round” — voqea. “It was my cat” — holat. Ko‘rdingizmi, yarmi voqea, yarmi fon. Shu aralashma hikoyani jonli qiladi.

Hikoya to‘rt qismdan iborat. Birinchi: fon — “It was raining, people were hurrying”. Ikkinchi: voqea — “Suddenly the lights went out”. Uchinchi: oqibat — “So we lit candles”. To‘rtinchi, ba’zan: oldingi sabab, bu past perfect: “I had forgotten to pay the bill”. Past perfect’ni bugun faqat tanib oling, uni keyinroq mashq qilamiz.

Hikoyani chiroyli qiladigan so‘zlar: suddenly, then, after that, finally — voqealar uchun. Meanwhile — shu orada, parallel jarayon uchun. Just then, at that moment, all of a sudden — kutilmagan payt uchun. Bu so‘zlar gapni bog‘laydi va hikoyaga ritm beradi. Ularni vergul bilan ajrating, masalan: “Suddenly, the door opened.”

Birinchi hikoyani tinglang. Sovuq juma kechasi. Fon past continuous’da: kutayotgan edim, odamlar shoshilayotgan edi. Keyin voqealar past simple’da: angladim, yugurib keldi. Oxirida past perfect: tushirib yuborgan edim. Hikoya qanday qurilganiga quloq soling.

To‘rtta savol. Ob-havo qanday edi? Gapiruvchi nima qilayotgan edi? U nimani angladi? Bola nimani ushlab turgan edi? To‘liq gap bilan, to‘g‘ri zamonda javob bering.

Javoblar. Bir — sovuq edi. Ikki — avtobus kutayotgan edi. Uch — hamyoni sumkada yo‘qligini angladi. To‘rt — bola hamyonni ushlab turgan edi. Ko‘rdingizmi: savolning zamoni javobning zamonini ham belgilaydi.

Ikkinchi dialog — odatiy suhbat. Birinchi odam kechagi paytni so‘raydi: “What were you doing at ten last night?” Ikkinchisi jarayon bilan javob beradi, keyin to‘xtatuvchi voqeani aytadi. E’tibor bering: “wearing a big hat” ham fon, “didn’t recognise” esa voqea.

To‘rtta savol. B soat o‘nda nima qilayotgan edi? U qayerda o‘tirgan edi? Jim nima kiyib olgan edi? B nega uni tanimadi?

Javoblar. Bir — bog‘da yurgan edi. Ikki — skameykada o‘tirgan edi. Uch — katta shlyapa kiygan edi. To‘rt — shlyapa tufayli. Bu hikoyada ham fon va voqea almashib turibdi, bu ingliz tilidagi og‘zaki hikoyaning asosiy uslubi.

Odatiy nutqda “was” kuchsiz — /wəz/, “were” esa /wə/. “I was walking” — /aɪ wəz ˈwɔːkɪŋ/. Lekin qisqa javobda ular kuchli: “Yes, I was” — /wɒz/. Shuningdek -ing oxirida g deyarli eshitilmaydi, burun tovushi /ŋ/ chiqadi. Asosiy fe’lga urg‘u beriladi, yordamchiga emas.

Takrorlang. Yordamchi fe’lni qisqa, kuchsiz aytib, urg‘uni asosiy so‘zga bering: WALKing, WAITing, DOing, RAINing. Tez va tekis ritm.

Tekshirib ko‘ring. Urg‘u WALK, WAIT, DO, RAIN bo‘g‘inlarida, was va were esa yengil. Agar “was” ni baland aytgan bo‘lsangiz, qaytadan urinib ko‘ring. Bu ritm ingliz tilini tabiiy qiladi.

Yana ikki xato. Birinchisi: was bilan oddiy fe’l: “I was go”. To‘g‘risi “I was going”, albatta -ing bilan. Ikkinchisi: while dan keyin past simple: “While I walked, I saw him”. Bu aslida mumkin, lekin ma’no boshqa. Jarayonni ko‘rsatish uchun “While I was walking” aytish tabiiyroq. Bu o‘zbek tilidagi “yurganimda” umumiy shaklidan o‘tgan odat.

Namuna hikoya. “It was a quiet morning and I was drinking tea.” — fon. “Suddenly, the lights went out.” — voqea. “I went outside, and the whole street was dark.” — oqibat. Oxirida qisqa xulosa. Sizning hikoyangiz ham shu tuzilishda bo‘lsin: fon, voqea, oqibat. Shunda bir daqiqada ham tartibli, ham qiziqarli gapirasiz.

Navbat sizda. O‘ttiz soniya ichida kutilmagan voqea haqida qisqa hikoya qiling. Fon bilan boshlang: “It was ... and I was ...”. Keyin “Suddenly” bilan voqeani ayting va oqibat bilan tugating. Past continuous va past simple’ni birga ishlating.

Namuna javob. Fon: issiq kun, bekatda kutayotgan edim. Voqea: to‘satdan it yugurib o‘tdi. Parallel jarayon: qizcha uning ketidan yugurayotgan edi. Oqibat: itni ushladim, qizcha rahmat dedi. Sizniki ham shu tartibda bo‘ldimi? Yana bir bor ayting, ikkinchi urinishda ancha ravon bo‘ladi.

Xulosa. Bir: past continuous — was yoki were va -ing. Ikki: u o‘tmishdagi aniq paytdagi jarayonni va hikoya fonini bildiradi. Uch: qisqa voqea past simple’da, uni to‘xtatgan jarayon past continuous’da. To‘rt: when odatda qisqa voqea bilan, while uzoq jarayon bilan. Besh: holat fe’llari continuous bo‘lmaydi. Shularni eslab qolsangiz, hikoyangiz jonli chiqadi.

Uy vazifasi: kutilmagan voqea haqida bir daqiqalik hikoya tayyorlang va ovoz yozib oling. Hikoyada fon past continuous’da, voqealar past simple’da bo‘lsin, when va while ishlating. Muvaffaqiyat mezoni: kamida to‘rtta past continuous va to‘rtta past simple gap. Keyingi darsda kelasi zamonning turli shakllarini o‘rganamiz.
