Ingliz tili. 61-dars. Ishga kirish suhbati

Assalomu alaykum, xush kelibsiz! Oldingi darsda fikr bildirishni va uni sabab hamda misol bilan asoslashni o‘rgandik. Bugun shu ko‘nikmani juda muhim vaziyatda qo‘llaymiz: ishga kirish suhbati. Dars oxirida o‘zingizni tanishtirasiz, kuchli tomonlaringiz va tajribangizni aniq tasvirlaysiz, rasmiy uslubda gapirasiz va suhbat oxirida o‘zingiz ham savol berasiz.

Ko‘pchilik ingliz tilidagi suhbatlar bir xil tartibda o‘tadi. Avval salomlashish va qisqa suhbat: “Thank you for coming”. Keyin sizning o‘zingiz haqingizdagi savollar, ish bo‘yicha savollar, so‘ng sizning savollaringiz va xayrlashuv. Tartibni bilsangiz, nimaga tayyor turishni bilasiz va xotirjam bo‘lasiz. Bugun har bir bosqichni o‘rganamiz, ayniqsa o‘zingiz haqingizda gapirish va savol berish qismlarini.

Eng birinchi savol deyarli doim shu: “Tell me about yourself”. Bu hayot hikoyangizni so‘rash emas. Qisqa, uch qismli javob bering: hozirgi holatingiz, o‘tgan tajribangiz va kelajak maqsadingiz. Masalan: “I’m an accountant with three years of experience. I studied economics in Tashkent. Now I’m looking for a more challenging role.”

Ikki xato. Birinchisi: javobni ism, yosh va sevimli mashg‘ulot bilan cheklash. Ish beruvchi sizdan kasbiy ma’lumot kutadi: nimani o‘qigansiz, qayerda ishlagansiz, nimaga qodirsiz. Ikkinchisi: “good in English”. O‘zbekchada “inglizchada yaxshi” deymiz, shuning uchun “in” ishlatamiz, ammo to‘g‘risi “good at”. Xuddi shuningdek, “experience in” yoki “experience of” ham ishlatiladi, lekin “good at” ga o‘zgarmaydi.

Endi o‘zingizni 3 gap bilan tanishtiring. Birinchi gap: hozir kimsiz yoki nima bilan shug‘ullanasiz. Ikkinchi: nimani o‘qigansiz yoki qayerda ishlagansiz, o‘tgan zamonda. Uchinchi: qanday lavozim qidiryapsiz. Agar ishlamasangiz, talaba bo‘lib ham javob bera olasiz. Baland ovozda ayting.

Namuna: “I’m a recent graduate in marketing. I studied at university in Tashkent and did an internship at a local company. Now I’m looking for a junior marketing position.” Ko‘rdingizmi, bu yerda hozirgi zamon, o‘tgan zamon va “looking for” bor. Hozir buni baland ovozda takrorlashingiz mumkin.

Ikkinchi klassik savol: “Why do you want this job?”. Yaxshi javob uchta narsani o‘z ichiga oladi: ish yoki kompaniyaga qiziqish, sizning tajribangiz bunga mos kelishi va nimani o‘rganmoqchi ekaningiz. Kalit iboralar: “I’m interested in”, “I’d like the opportunity to”. Kechagi darsdagidek “because” bilan sabab keltiring. “Because I need money” deb javob bermang, bu rost bo‘lsa ham ish beruvchiga yoqmaydi.

Bu savol qo‘pol tuyulishi mumkin, ammo aslida u sizga o‘zingizni sotish imkonini beradi. Maqtanmasdan, aniq bo‘ling: “I have the skills you need”, “I’m a quick learner”, “I can bring fresh ideas to the team”. Har gapdan keyin qisqa dalil qo‘shing. Masalan, “I’m a quick learner. In my last job, I learned the new system in a week.”

Kuchli tomonlarni aytish uchun sifatlar kerak: “reliable” ishonchli, “organised” tartibli, “flexible” moslashuvchan, “hard-working” mehnatsevar, “creative” ijodkor. Yana “a good communicator” va “a team player”. “Good at” dan keyin fe’lning -ing shakli keladi: “good at solving problems”. Faqat sifatni aytish yetarli emas, albatta misol ham keltiring.

Eng yaxshi javob qolipi: kuchli tomon, keyin misol, keyin natija. “I’m reliable” bu da’vo. “I haven’t missed a day of work in two years” bu dalil. Yoki: “I led a small team and we finished the project early”. Dalil har doim aniq va tekshirib bo‘ladigan bo‘lsin, shunda ishonarli eshitiladi.

“What is your weakness?” savolidan qo‘rqmang. Maqsad sizni yo‘qotish emas, o‘zingizni bilishingizni ko‘rsatish. Qoida: haqiqiy, lekin ishga halaqit bermaydigan kichik kamchilikni ayting va uni qanday yaxshilayotganingizni qo‘shing. “I’m working on my public speaking” yoki “I used to find presentations difficult, but now I practise every week”. “I have no weaknesses” deb javob bermang, bu ishonchsiz eshitiladi.

Ikki xato. Birinchisi “good in”, to‘g‘risi “good at”. Ikkinchisi: “a big experience”. O‘zbekchada “katta tajriba” deymiz, lekin inglizchada “experience” bu ma’noda sanalmaydigan ot, shuning uchun artikl va ko‘plik yo‘q: “a lot of experience”, “five years of experience”. Faqat konkret voqea ma’nosida sanaladi: “It was a great experience”. Yana bir xato: “I am hard-worker”. To‘g‘risi: “I am hard-working” yoki “I am a hard worker”.

Endi o‘zingiz urinib ko‘ring. Bitta kuchli tomonni tanlang, masalan ishonchli yoki tartibli. Ayting: “I’m …” va unga bitta misol qo‘shing. Keyin bitta kichik kamchilikni ayting va uni yaxshilash uchun nima qilayotganingizni qo‘shing. Jami to‘rt gap. Ish tajribangiz bo‘lmasa, o‘qish yoki uy hayotidan misol keltiring.

Namunaviy javob: “I’m very organised. I plan my week every Sunday, and I have never missed a deadline at university. My weakness is that I sometimes find it hard to say no, so I’m learning to set priorities.” Bu yerda kuchli tomon dalil bilan keldi, kamchilik esa yechim bilan.

Tajribani tasvirlashda ikkita zamon asosiy. Tugagan davr uchun o‘tgan oddiy zamon: “I worked at a bank from 2019 to 2022”. Hali davom etayotgan ish uchun hozirgi tugallangan zamon: “I’ve worked as a teacher for five years”. “For” davomiylikni ko‘rsatadi, “since” boshlanish nuqtasini: “since 2021”.

Ishdagi vazifalaringizni aytish uchun yaxshi fe’llar bor: “managed” boshqargan, “organised” tashkil qilgan, “trained” o‘rgatgan, “developed” rivojlantirgan, “dealt with” bilan ishlagan. Iboralar: “I was responsible for” va “My main duties included”. “Responsible for” dan keyin ot yoki -ing shakli keladi: “responsible for managing the website”.

Uchta xato. Birinchisi: “I work here since 2019”. “Since” bilan hozirgi oddiy zamon ishlatilmaydi, hozirgi tugallangan zamon kerak: “I’ve worked here since 2019”. O‘zbekchada “2019 dan beri ishlayman” deymiz, shuning uchun xato qilinadi. Ikkinchisi: aniq o‘tgan vaqt bilan, “in 2019”, present perfect emas, o‘tgan oddiy zamon: “I worked there in 2019”. Uchinchisi: “responsible for”, “of” emas.

Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklga qo‘ying. Birinchi: “I work here since 2020”. Ikkinchi: “I work at a shop in 2018”. Uchinchi: “I be a manager for two years now”. Har bir gapda “since”, “in 2018” va “for” ko‘rsatkichlariga qarab zamonni tanlang. Baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar: birinchi, “I’ve worked here since 2020”, chunki “since” va ish hozir ham davom etyapti. Ikkinchi, “I worked at a shop in 2018”: aniq o‘tgan yil, shuning uchun o‘tgan oddiy zamon. Uchinchi, “I’ve been a manager for two years”: “be” ning hozirgi tugallangan shakli “have been”.

Suhbat rasmiy vaziyat, shuning uchun uslubni moslang. “Yeah” o‘rniga “yes”, “nope” o‘rniga “no”, “guys” o‘rniga ismlarni ishlating. Qisqartmalar masalan “I’m” va “I’ve” aytishga yo‘l qo‘yiladi, lekin “gonna” va “wanna” ishlatmang. Savolni tushunmasangiz, uyalmang: “Could you repeat the question, please?”. Bu muloyim eshitiladi.

Mana norasmiy va rasmiyroq variantlar. “I want” o‘rniga “I would like”. “A lot of” o‘rniga ba’zan “a great deal of”. “Get a job” o‘rniga “find a position”. “Sorry?” o‘rniga “Pardon?” yoki “Could you say that again?”. “I would like to work here” ishonchli eshitiladi, “I want to work here” esa biroz to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri.

Ikki juda keng tarqalgan xato. Birinchisi: norasmiy so‘zlar, “yeah”, “no problem”, “whatever”. Ularning o‘rniga “Yes, certainly” yoki “Of course”. Ikkinchisi: oldingi ish joyi yoki rahbar haqida salbiy gapirish. “I hate my old boss” deb aytsangiz, kelajakda sizning ham shunday gapirishingizni o‘ylashadi. Shunday ekan, ishdan ketish sababini ijobiy ifodalang: “I wanted a new challenge” yoki “I was looking for more opportunities to grow”.

Uchta gapni rasmiy suhbatga mos qilib qayta ayting. Birinchi: “Yeah, I wanna work here”. Ikkinchi: “Sorry? Say it again!”. Uchinchi: “My last boss was awful, so I left”. Uchinchisini ijobiy sababga aylantiring. Baland ovozda ayting. Ma’no saqlansin, faqat uslub o‘zgarsin.

Javoblar: birinchi, “Yes, I would like to work here”. Ikkinchi, “Could you repeat that, please?”. Uchinchi, “I wanted a new challenge, so I left”. Ko‘rdingizmi, ma’no saqlanadi, faqat ohang muloyim va professional bo‘ldi. Shu uslubni suhbatda ham ishlating.

Suhbatda savolni tushunmay qolish mumkin, bu normal. Jim turmang va taxmin bilan javob bermang. Muloyim ayting: “Could you repeat that, please?” yoki “Could you explain what you mean by…?”. O‘ylash vaqti kerak bo‘lsa: “That’s a good question. Let me think for a moment.” Bu iboralar sizni tayyorgarliksiz emas, aksincha, ehtiyotkor va ishonchli ko‘rsatadi.

Birinchi dialogni tinglang. Menejer Ms Clark nomzod Dilshod bilan suhbatlashmoqda. Savollar: Dilshodning kasbi nima, qancha tajribasi bor va asosiy kuchli tomoni nima? Dilshod qanday rasmiy iboralarni ishlatayotganiga ham e’tibor bering. Tinglagandan so‘ng savollarga javob berasiz.

Uchta savol. Birinchi: Dilshodning kasbi nima? Ikkinchi: u hozirgi kompaniyada qachondan beri ishlaydi? Uchinchi: uning kuchli tomoni nima? To‘liq gap bilan javob bering va zamonni to‘g‘ri tanlang: ikkinchi javobda “since” bor. Dilshod gaplarini diqqat bilan eslang.

Javoblar: birinchi, Dilshod veb-dasturchi. Ikkinchi, “He has worked there since 2021”, hozirgi tugallangan zamon va “since”. Uchinchi, uning kuchli tomoni: tez o‘rganadi. Dilshod savolni tinglab, aniq javob berdi, “certainly” deb muloyim boshladi va oxirida kelajak maqsadini qo‘shdi.

Suhbat oxirida deyarli har doim so‘rashadi: “Do you have any questions for us?”. “No” deb javob berish zaif taassurot qoldiradi. Tayyorlab boring! Yaxshi savollar: “What would a typical day look like?”, “What are the opportunities for training?”, “What are the next steps?”.

Bu bosqichda ham xato bor. Birinchisi: suhbat boshida yoki birinchi savol sifatida maosh va ta’tilni so‘rash. Bu ishni emas, faqat pulni o‘ylayotganingizni ko‘rsatadi. Maosh haqida odatda ish beruvchi o‘zi gapiradi. Ikkinchisi: kompaniya sayti va ochiq ma’lumotlarda bor narsani so‘rash. Oldindan o‘qib keling. Uchinchisi: grammatik xato, “What time you start?”. Savolda yordamchi fe’l kerak: “What time do you start?”.

Uchta mavzu bo‘yicha savol tuzing. Birinchi: bu lavozimdagi odatiy kun. Ikkinchi: o‘qish va rivojlanish imkoniyatlari. Uchinchi: keyingi bosqichlar. Har birini muloyim va to‘liq savol shaklida ayting. Masalan, “Could you tell me…” bilan boshlasangiz ham bo‘ladi.

Namunaviy savollar: birinchi, “What would a typical day look like in this role?”. Ikkinchi, “What training opportunities are there?”. Uchinchi, “Could you tell me about the next steps?”. Oxirgisida “Could you tell me” dan keyin odatiy tartib qaytadi, ya’ni “about the next steps” deyiladi.

Ikkinchi dialog suhbatning oxiri. Nomzod Madina savol beradi va suhbatni muloyim tugatadi. Savollar: Madina nima haqida so‘radi, dastur qancha davom etadi va qachon javob olishadi? Uning rahmat aytish iboralariga ham e’tibor bering, ular suhbatni muloyim yakunlaydi.

Uchta savol. Birinchi: Madina nima haqida so‘radi? Ikkinchi: dastur qancha davom etadi? Uchinchi: ular Madina bilan qachon bog‘lanadi? To‘liq gapda javob bering. Esingizda bo‘lsin, yakunda “look forward to hearing from you” iborasi ishlatildi. Dialogni ichingizda yana bir bor eslab chiqing.

Javoblar: birinchi, Madina o‘qish yoki trening haqida so‘radi. Ikkinchi, dastur ikki hafta davom etadi. Uchinchi, ular kelasi hafta bog‘lanadi. Madina oxirida “Thank you very much for your time” va “I look forward to hearing from you” dedi. Bu ikki ibora suhbatni chiroyli yakunlaydi. “Look forward to” dan keyin fe’lning -ing shakli keladi, “hearing”.

Suhbatda aniq talaffuz ishonchni oshiradi. So‘z urg‘usi: “interview” da birinchi bo‘g‘in, ya’ni “IN-ter-view”. “Experience” da ikkinchi: “ex-PE-ri-ence”. “Responsible” ham ikkinchi: “re-SPON-si-ble”. “Career” da ham ikkinchi bo‘g‘in: “ka-REER”. Ohangda esa darak gapni pasayuvchi tonda ayting, bu ishonchli eshitiladi.

To‘rtta so‘zni to‘g‘ri urg‘u bilan ayting. Avval “interview”, birinchi bo‘g‘inga urg‘u. Keyin “experience”, “responsible” va “career”, ularda ikkinchi bo‘g‘in kuchli. Keyin to‘liq gapda ayting: “I have experience in this career”. Ikki marta takrorlang.

Tekshiramiz. “Interview” da urg‘u birinchi bo‘g‘inda, “exPErience” da ikkinchisida, “reSPONsible” da ikkinchisida va “caREER” da ham ikkinchisida. Ko‘p o‘zbek o‘quvchilar “interVIEW” deb aytadi, chunki so‘zning oxiriga urg‘u berish o‘zbekchada ko‘p uchraydi.

Endi to‘liq mashq. Beshta savolga ovoz chiqarib javob bering. Birinchi: o‘zingiz haqingizda. Ikkinchi: nega bu ish. Uchinchi: kuchli tomonlaringiz. To‘rtinchi: tajribangiz. Beshinchi: o‘z savolingiz. Har biriga kamida ikki gap ayting. Rasmiy, aniq va xotirjam gapiring.

Namunaviy javob qismlari. “I’m a recent marketing graduate and I did an internship at a local company.” “I’m interested in this role because it combines creativity and data.” “I’m reliable: I have never missed a deadline.” “I’ve worked on three campaigns.” “Could you tell me about training opportunities?” Ko‘rdingizmi, har javob qisqa, aniq va dalilli. Uy vazifasida aynan shu uslubni takrorlaysiz.

Xulosa qilamiz. O‘zingiz haqingizda: hozir, o‘tmish va maqsad. Kuchli tomonni doim misol bilan ayting. Tajribada: tugagan ish uchun o‘tgan zamon, davom etayotgan ish uchun hozirgi tugallangan zamon, “since” va “for”. Rasmiy uslubda “yeah” va “gonna” dan qoching, oldingi rahbarni tanqid qilmang. Va oxirida kamida ikkita tayyor savol bering. Esda tuting: “good at”, “a lot of experience”, “responsible for”.

Uy vazifasi: beshta suhbat savoliga ovoz chiqarib javob bering: o‘zingiz haqingizda, nega bu ish, kuchli tomonlar, tajriba va o‘zingizning savolingiz. O‘zingizni yozib oling. Mezon: har javob kamida ikki gap, kuchli tomonda misol bor, rasmiy uslub saqlangan, “good at” va “since” to‘g‘ri ishlatilgan. Keyingi darsda ta’lim va o‘qish mavzusini o‘rganamiz.
