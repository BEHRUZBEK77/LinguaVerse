Ingliz tili. 66-dars. Ko‘chirma gap asoslari

Assalomu alaykum! Yangi darsga xush kelibsiz. Oldingi darsda atrof-muhit va ekologiya haqida gaplashdik. Bugun esa boshqa odamlarning gapini yetkazishni o‘rganamiz. Dars oxirida bugun bo‘lgan suhbatni ingliz tilida to‘g‘ri va tabiiy qilib hikoya qila olasiz.

Birinchi tushuncha. Biror kishining so‘zini aynan o‘zicha keltirsak, bu to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri gap: tirnoq ichida, kishining o‘z ovozi bilan. Mazmunini o‘z so‘zimiz bilan yetkazsak, bu ko‘chirma gap, ingliz tilida reported speech. Unda tirnoq yo‘q, “said” fe’lidan keyin esa ko‘pincha “that” keladi.

O‘zbek tilida ko‘chirma gap oson: “Men charchadim”, deb aytdi, yoki “charchaganini aytdi”. Fe’l zamoni deyarli o‘zgarmaydi. Ingliz tilida esa qoida boshqacha: gapni o‘tgan zamonda yetkazganimizda, ichidagi fe’l ham, odatda, bir pog‘ona o‘tmishga siljiydi. Bu siljish backshift deyiladi.

Qoidaning birinchi qismi: hozirgi zamon o‘tgan zamonga aylanadi. “Am, is, are” bo‘ladi “was, were”. Present simple, masalan “I work”, bo‘ladi past simple, “she worked”. Present continuous, “we are waiting”, bo‘ladi past continuous, “they were waiting”.

Ikkinchi qism. Present perfect bo‘ladi past perfect: “I’ve finished” — “he had finished”. Past simple ham ko‘pincha past perfect bo‘ladi: “I met her” — “he had met her”. Kelasi zamon: “will” bo‘ladi “would”, “can” bo‘ladi “could”. “Must” esa ko‘pincha “had to” ga aylanadi.

Yaxshi xabar: ba’zi fe’llar o‘zgarmaydi. Allaqachon o‘tmish shaklidagi modallar, ya’ni “would, could, should, might” va “ought to” o‘z holicha qoladi. Past perfect, ya’ni “had” plus uchinchi shakl, ham o‘zgarmaydi. Demak, “You should rest” ko‘chirma gapda ham “should” bo‘lib qoladi.

Eng keng tarqalgan xato — siljishni unutish. O‘zbek tilida zamon o‘zgarmagani uchun “He said he is tired” deyishga ko‘nikamiz. Bu yerda ikki xato aralashib ketadi: biz o‘tmishda aytilganini yetkazyapmiz, shuning uchun ingliz tili “was” ni talab qiladi. Xuddi shunday “She said she will come” noto‘g‘ri, to‘g‘risi “She said she would come”. Kelasi zamon ham orqaga suriladi.

Mashq vaqti. Har bir gapni “She said” bilan boshlab ko‘chirma gapga aylantiring. Birinchi: “I live in Tashkent”. Ikkinchi: “We are studying”. Uchinchi: “I’ll help you”. Olmoshlar haqida hozircha o‘ylamang, faqat fe’lni o‘zgartiring. Baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “She said she lived in Tashkent”, present simple past simple bo‘ldi. Ikkinchi: “She said they were studying”, “we” ning o‘rniga “they” keldi, buni birozdan keyin ko‘ramiz, fe’l esa past continuous bo‘ldi. Uchinchi: “She said she would help me”, “will” ning o‘rniga “would”, “you” esa “me” ga aylandi. Agar fe’llarni to‘g‘ri siljitgan bo‘lsangiz, barakalla.

Endi olmoshlar. Gapni boshqa odam nomidan yetkazamiz, shuning uchun “I” gapirgan kishiga qarab “he” yoki “she” bo‘ladi. “We” bo‘ladi “they”, “my” bo‘ladi “his” yoki “her”. Eng muhimi, o‘zingizdan so‘rang: bu gapni kim kimga aytdi? Agar o‘zingiz gapirgan bo‘lsangiz, “I” o‘z holicha qoladi.

Vaqt va joy so‘zlari ham o‘zgaradi, chunki hikoya qilayotgan vaqtimiz va joyimiz boshqa. “Now” bo‘ladi “then”, “today” bo‘ladi “that day”. “Yesterday” — “the day before”, “tomorrow” — “the next day” yoki “the following day”. “Here” bo‘ladi “there”, “this” bo‘ladi “that”, “ago” bo‘ladi “before”. “Next week” esa “the following week”.

Yana bir xato: “tomorrow” ni o‘zgartirmasdan qoldirish. Tasavvur qiling, dushanba kuni biror kishi “Ertaga kelaman” dedi. Siz esa payshanba kuni “He said he would come tomorrow” desangiz, ma’no buziladi: payshanbaga nisbatan ertaga emas edi. Shuning uchun “the next day” deymiz.

Endi hammasini birgalikda: fe’l, olmosh va vaqt so‘zlari. Birinchi: Ali, “I’m busy today”. Ikkinchi: Dilya, “We moved here last year”. Uchinchi: Tom, “I’ll call you tomorrow”, bu yerda “you” ni “me” deb oling. Har birini “said” bilan boshlang va baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “Ali said he was busy that day”: “I” ning o‘rniga “he”, “am” ning o‘rniga “was”, “today” ning o‘rniga “that day”. Ikkinchi: “Dilya said they had moved there the year before”: past simple past perfect bo‘ldi, “here” — “there”, “last year” — “the year before”. Uchinchi: “Tom said he would call me the next day”.

Endi fe’llar. “Say” va “tell” ikkalasi ham “aytmoq”, lekin ishlatilishi farq qiladi. “Tell” dan keyin albatta kimga aytilgani keladi: “told me, told him, told the team”. “Say” dan keyin odam kelmaydi: “said that”. Agar kimga aytilganini ko‘rsatmoqchi bo‘lsangiz, “said to me” deysiz.

Ikkita klassik xato. Birinchisi: “He said me”. Bunday ibora yo‘q: odam bo‘lsa, “told me” yoki “said to me” deyiladi. Ikkinchisi: “He told that he was ill”. Bu ham noto‘g‘ri, chunki “tell” dan keyin odam kerak: “He told us that he was ill” yoki “He said that he was ill”. Sodda qoida: odam bor bo‘lsa — tell, odam yo‘q bo‘lsa — say.

Bo‘sh joylarga “said” yoki “told” ni qo‘ying. Birinchi: “He blank me he was late”. Ikkinchi: “She blank that she was hungry”. Uchinchi: “They blank us the shop was closed”. Har bir gapda bo‘sh joydan keyin odam bormi yoki yo‘qmi, shunga qarang. Esda tuting: “told” dan keyin doim me, him, us kabi odam keladi, “said” dan keyin esa to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri gap boshlanadi. Baland ovozda aytib ko‘ring.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “told”, chunki keyin “me” bor. Ikkinchi: “said”, chunki keyin “that” keladi va odam yo‘q. Uchinchi: “told”, chunki keyin “us”. Yodlash uchun oddiy usul: “told” ning orqasidan odam, “said” ning orqasidan gap. Bu qoida sizni xatodan saqlaydi.

Uchinchi fe’l — “ask”, so‘ramoq. Savollarni yetkazganda ikki tur bor. Ha yoki yo‘q javobli savolda “if” yoki “whether” ishlatamiz: “She asked me if I liked tea”. So‘roq so‘zli savolda esa shu so‘roq so‘zning o‘zi qoladi: “He asked where I lived”. Zamon qoidasi o‘sha-o‘sha: bir qadam orqaga. Savol belgisi esa yo‘qoladi, chunki gap endi oddiy darak gapga aylandi.

Eng muhim qoida: ko‘chirma savolda so‘z tartibi darak gapnikidek, ya’ni ega, keyin kesim. Yordamchi “do, does, did” butunlay tushib qoladi, fe’lning o‘zi siljiydi. “What time does it start” — “what time it started”. “Are you ready” — “if I was ready”, bu yerda “are” ega tomonidan orqaga o‘tib, “was” bo‘ladi. Savolning o‘zida tartib teskari, ko‘chirma gapda esa to‘g‘ri.

Bu darajadagi eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato: ko‘chirma gapda savol tartibini saqlab qolish. “She asked me where did I live” noto‘g‘ri. To‘g‘risi “where I lived”. Ha yoki yo‘q savollarda ham xuddi shu: “He asked me do I smoke” noto‘g‘ri, to‘g‘risi “if I smoked”. “If” yoki “whether” unutilmasligi kerak.

Uchta savolni “She asked me” bilan boshlab yetkazing. Birinchi: “Do you speak French?” Ikkinchi: “Where did you buy it?” Uchinchi: “Can you help me?” Eslang: ha yoki yo‘q savolda “if”, savol tartibi yo‘q, siljish bor. Birinchi va uchinchi savolda “you” ni “I” ga, ikkinchida ham shunday o‘zgartiring, uchinchida “me” ni “her” deb oling.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “She asked me if I spoke French”. “Do” tushdi, “speak” siljib “spoke” bo‘ldi. Ikkinchi: “She asked me where I had bought it”: past simple past perfect bo‘ldi, “did” tushdi. Uchinchi: “She asked me if I could help her”, “can” bo‘ldi “could”, “me” esa “her”, chunki bu yerda yordam so‘rayotgan ayol. Uchalasida ham savol belgisi yo‘q.

Buyruq va iltimoslar ham ko‘chirma gapda yetkaziladi. Bu yerda zamon siljishi yo‘q: “tell” yoki “ask”, keyin odam, keyin “to” va fe’l. “Wait here” — “He told me to wait there”. Manfiy shaklda “not to”: “She asked us not to be late”. Buyruq uchun “tell”, muloyim iltimos uchun “ask”.

Tabiiy nutqda faqat “said” va “told” bilan cheklanmaymiz. “Explain” — tushuntirmoq, “add” — qo‘shib aytmoq, “reply” — javob bermoq, “mention” — tilga olmoq, “promise” — va’da bermoq, “admit” — tan olmoq, “warn” — ogohlantirmoq. Bularning ko‘pchiligidan keyin ham “that” va siljigan zamon keladi.

Birinchi tinglash dialogi. Ish joyi: ofis menejeri uchrashuvga kela olmaydi. Ali unga qo‘ng‘iroq qiladi. Tinglang va menejer nima uchun kela olmayotganini, qayerda ekanini hamda soat o‘n ikkida nima qilishini tushunishga harakat qiling. Keyin sizga savollar beraman.

Endi dialogni ko‘chirma gapda eslab ko‘ring. Birinchi: menejer qayerda edi? Ikkinchi: u nima uchun kela olmadi? Uchinchi: u soat o‘n ikkida nima qilishini aytdi? Javoblarni “She said” bilan boshlang, shunda siljishni mashq qilasiz. Menejerning o‘z so‘zlarini emas, mazmunini ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “She said she was at the airport”. Ikkinchi: “She said her flight had been late” — reys oldin kechikkan, shuning uchun past perfect. Uchinchi: “She said she would join them online at noon”, ya’ni soat o‘n ikkida onlayn qo‘shilaman. E’tibor bering: Ali ham gap aytdi, “Don’t worry” — “He told her not to worry”, bu esa buyruq-ko‘chirma shakli.

Ikkinchi dialog. Ikki do‘st, Sara va Tom, kechki bayram haqida gaplashadi. Sara Tomni taklif qiladi, Tom esa bandligini aytadi. Tinglang: Tom nima uchun bora olmaydi va nimani o‘qiyapti? Qisqartmalarga, ya’ni “can’t”, “I’m” va “I’ll” ga ham quloq soling.

Yana ko‘chirma gap mashqi. Birinchi: Sara Tomdan birinchi navbatda nimani so‘radi? Ikkinchi: Tom nima qilishi kerakligini aytdi? Uchinchi: Tom keyingi safar nima qilishini aytdi? Birinchi javobni “She asked him if” bilan boshlang, ikkinchisini “He said”, uchinchisini ham “He said” bilan.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “She asked him if he was coming to the party that night” — “are you coming” bo‘ldi “he was coming”, “tonight” esa “that night”. Ikkinchi: “He said he had to study”. Bu yerda “have to” siljib “had to” bo‘ldi. Uchinchi: “He said he would come next time” yoki “the next time”.

Talaffuz haqida. “Said” so‘zi yozilishiga qaramay “seid” emas, “sed” deb o‘qiladi, bu “say” fe’lining o‘tgan shakli, shakli “says” ham “sez” bo‘ladi. “Asked” oxirida “kt” jarangsiz birikma, “askt” deb o‘qiladi, “ed” alohida bo‘g‘in emas. Og‘zaki nutqda yana bir narsa: “he’d” qisqartmasi. U “he had” yoki “he would” degani. Gapdan ma’no tushunasiz: “he’d eaten” bo‘lsa, “had”; “he’d come” bo‘lsa, “would”.

Muhim istisno. Agar siz gapni hozirgi zamonda yetkazsangiz, ya’ni “says” yoki “tells” desangiz, zamon siljimaydi. Masalan, telefonda sherigingiz “Men charchadim” dedi va siz darhol “She says she’s tired” deysiz. Mantiq oddiy: gap hozir ham dolzarb. O‘tgan zamon “said” bo‘lgandagina orqaga siljiymiz.

Yana bir nuqta. Agar aytilgan gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ingliz tilida ba’zan zamonni siljitmaslik mumkin: “She said she lives in Leeds”, agar u hozir ham Lidsda yashasa. Lekin siljigan shakl, “she lived in Leeds”, ham doim to‘g‘ri. Shuning uchun imtihonda yoki shubhada doim siljitgan shaklni tanlang, bu xavfsiz yo‘l.

Uchta gapda xato bor. Birinchi: “He said me he was tired”. Ikkinchi: “She asked where did I work”. Uchinchi: “They said they will come tomorrow”, bu gap o‘tgan hafta aytilgan edi deb tasavvur qiling. Har bir xatoni toping va to‘g‘ri variantni ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “He told me he was tired”, chunki “said me” yo‘q. Ikkinchi: “She asked where I worked”, savol tartibi va “did” tushdi. Uchinchi: “They said they would come the next day”, chunki “will” siljib “would” bo‘ldi va “tomorrow” o‘zgardi. Uchalasini topgan bo‘lsangiz, bugungi darsning eng muhim xatolaridan qutuldingiz.

Endi suhbatni hikoya qilishni o‘rganamiz. Barcha gaplarni “said that” bilan ketma-ket qo‘ysangiz, nutqingiz zerikarli chiqadi. Shuning uchun tartib so‘zlarini qo‘shing: “First, then, after that, in the end”. Fe’llarni almashtiring: “asked, told, explained, added, promised”.

Mana namuna. Bugun ertalab rahbarim mendan kechroq ishlay olamanmi, deb so‘radi. Men darsim borligini aytdim, u esa muammo yo‘qligini bildirdi va juma kuni erta ketishim mumkinligini qo‘shib qo‘ydi. Namunada ko‘chirma savol, “told”, “said” va “added” fe’llari qanday ishlatilganini payqang.

Endi sizning navbatingiz. Bugun yoki kecha qilgan bitta suhbatingizni eslang: oila a’zosi, hamkasb yoki do‘kondagi sotuvchi bilan. Kamida to‘rtta ko‘chirma gap ayting: bittasida “asked”, bittasida “told”, bittasida “said” bo‘lsin, tartib so‘zlaridan foydalaning. To‘xtatib, o‘zingiz gapiring.

Endi o‘zingizni tekshiring. Birinchi: fe’l zamoni bir qadam orqaga siljidimi? Ikkinchi: olmoshlar, “tomorrow”, “here” kabi so‘zlar o‘zgardimi? Uchinchi: savolda “if” yoki so‘roq so‘z va darak gap tartibi bormi? To‘rtinchi: “told” dan keyin odam bormi?

Xulosa qilamiz. Birinchi: o‘tgan zamondagi “said” dan keyin zamon bir qadam orqaga siljiydi: hozirgi o‘tgan, “will” — “would”, “can” — “could”. Ikkinchi: olmoshlar, vaqt va joy so‘zlari ham o‘zgaradi. Uchinchi: “told” dan keyin odam keladi, “said” dan keyin yo‘q. To‘rtinchi: ko‘chirma savolda “if” yoki so‘roq so‘z, so‘ng darak gap tartibi, “do” va savol belgisisiz. Beshinchi: “says” bilan siljish yo‘q.

Uy vazifasi: bugun qilgan suhbatingizni yozma yoki og‘zaki hikoya qiling. Kamida beshta ko‘chirma gap bo‘lsin, ulardan bittasi ko‘chirma savol, bittasi “told”, bittasida “said”. Muvaffaqiyat mezoni: har bir gapda zamon siljigan, olmoshlar to‘g‘ri va savolda darak tartibi bor. Keyingi darsda fe’lning noaniq shakllarini, ya’ni infinitiv va harakat nomini o‘rganamiz.
