Ingliz tili. 67-dars. Fe’lning noaniq shakllari

Assalomu alaykum! Oldingi darsda boshqalarning gapini ko‘chirma gapda yetkazishni o‘rgandik. Bugun har bir gapda uchraydigan mavzu: ikki fe’l yonma-yon kelganda ikkinchisi qanday shaklda bo‘ladi? “I want to go” deymizmi yoki “I want going”? Dars oxirida eng keng tarqalgan fe’l qoliplarini bilasiz, maqsadni to‘g‘ri ifodalaysiz va tipik xatolardan qochasiz.

Ingliz tilida ikkinchi fe’l ikki shaklda keladi. “To” bilan, masalan “to learn”, bu infinitiv. Yoki “-ing” bilan, masalan “learning”, bu gerund, ya’ni harakat nomi. O‘zbek tilida ikkalasi ham “-moq” yoki “-ish” bilan ifodalanadi: “o‘rganmoq”, “o‘rganish”. Shuning uchun tanlov o‘zbek tilidan ko‘rinmaydi. Birinchi fe’lning o‘ziga qarab tanlanadi, shu sababli qolipni yodlash kerak.

Birinchi guruh: infinitiv bilan keladigan fe’llar. Odatda ular kelajakka, niyatga, rejaga yo‘naltirilgan: “want, need, hope, plan, decide, promise, agree, refuse, offer, expect”. Yana “learn, manage, afford, choose”. Masalan, “manage to do” — uddalamoq, “afford to buy” — sotib olishga qurbi yetmoq. Mantiq shunday: “to” yo‘nalishni bildiradi, ya’ni hali amalga oshmagan ish sari qadam.

Ikkinchi guruh: “-ing” bilan keladigan fe’llar: “enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, miss, suggest, keep, practise, consider, imagine, give up”. Ular odatda amalning o‘zini, ko‘pincha allaqachon bo‘lgan yoki umuman faoliyatni bildiradi. “Do you mind opening the window?” — muloyim iltimos, “derazani ochsangiz bo‘ladimi?”. Ingliz tilida “practise” fe’l, Amerikada esa “practice” deb yoziladi.

Eng ko‘p xatolar shu yerda. “Enjoy” dan keyin “to” qo‘yib bo‘lmaydi: “I enjoy to read” noto‘g‘ri, to‘g‘risi “I enjoy reading”. “Want” dan keyin esa “-ing” kelmaydi: “wants to go”. “Suggest” ayniqsa ayyor: o‘zbekcha “borishni taklif qildi” deymiz, shuning uchun “suggested to go” deyishga moyil bo‘lamiz. To‘g‘risi “suggested going”. Har bir yangi fe’lni qolibi bilan birga yodlang.

Mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklga qo‘ying. Birinchi: “I decided, take, a taxi”. Ikkinchi: “She avoids, eat, sugar”. Uchinchi: “They hope, win, the match”. Birinchi fe’lga qarang: u infinitiv guruhidami yoki gerund guruhidami? Baland ovozda to‘liq gapni ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “I decided to take a taxi”, chunki “decide” infinitiv bilan keladi. Ikkinchi: “She avoids eating sugar”, “avoid” gerund guruhida. Uchinchi: “They hope to win the match”, “hope” infinitiv bilan. Agar xato qilgan bo‘lsangiz, o‘zingizni koyimang: aynan shu ro‘yxatni takrorlash bilan qoliplar xotirada o‘rnashadi.

Yaxshi xabar: ba’zi fe’llar ikkala shaklni qabul qiladi va ma’no deyarli o‘zgarmaydi. Bular “like, love, hate, prefer” va “start, begin, continue”. “It started raining” va “It started to rain” bir xil. Bir nozik tafovut bor: “like swimming” umuman yoqtirishni, “like to swim” esa odat yoki tanlovni bildirishi mumkin. Lekin amalda farqni sezmasangiz ham bo‘ladi.

Endi ma’no o‘zgaradigan fe’llar. “Remember to do” — qilishni esda tutmoq, hali qilinmagan ish: “Remember to lock the door”. “Remember doing” — o‘tmishda qilganingizni eslamoq: “I remember locking the door”, ya’ni qulflaganim esimda. “Forget” ham shunday: “I forgot to call” — qo‘ng‘iroq qilishni unutdim, “I’ll never forget meeting her” — u bilan uchrashganimni hech qachon unutmayman.

“Stop” bilan ehtiyot bo‘ling. “He stopped to buy water” — u suv olish uchun to‘xtadi, ya’ni to‘xtashning maqsadi shu. “He stopped smoking” — u chekishni tashladi. “Try” ham ikki xil: “try to do” — qiyin ishni uddalashga urinmoq, “try doing” — natijani ko‘rish uchun sinab ko‘rmoq. “Try restarting the computer” — kompyuterni qayta yoqib ko‘ring, balki ishlab ketar.

Bu yerda ma’no butunlay o‘zgarib ketadi. Agar “chekishni tashladim” demoqchi bo‘lsangiz va “I stopped to smoke” desangiz, inglizlar “chekish uchun to‘xtadim” deb tushunadi. To‘g‘risi “I stopped smoking”. Xuddi shunday, “uchrashishni unutdim” degani “I forgot to meet”, “I forgot meeting” esa “uchrashganimni unutdim” bo‘ladi. Xatoning narxi: boshqa ma’no.

Mashq. Birinchi: “Remember, buy, the tickets” — hali olinmagan chiptalar. Ikkinchi: “I remember, see, this film as a child” — bolaligimda ko‘rganim esimda. Uchinchi: “She stopped, have, a coffee” — u qahva ichish uchun to‘xtadi. Har bir gapning ma’nosiga qarab infinitiv yoki gerund tanlang.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “Remember to buy”, chunki bu kelajakdagi vazifa. Ikkinchi: “I remember seeing”, chunki bu o‘tmishdagi xotira. Uchinchi: “She stopped to have a coffee”, chunki to‘xtashdan maqsad qahva ichish. Qoida oddiy: hali bo‘lmagan ish va maqsad uchun infinitiv, o‘tmishdagi yoki davom etgan ish uchun gerund.

Endi maqsad. “Nima uchun?” degan savolga infinitiv bilan javob beramiz: “I went to the bank to get money”. O‘zbek tilida “pul olish uchun” deymiz, ingliz tilida “for” kerak emas. Rasmiyroq uslubda “in order to”. Inkor maqsadda “so as not to” yoki “in order not to”: “Speak quietly so as not to wake the baby”. Oddiy “not to” ham mumkin, lekin “so as not to” ancha aniq.

Maqsadda eng ko‘p xato: “for” ni infinitiv oldidan qo‘yish. “I came here for to study” noto‘g‘ri, to‘g‘risi “to study”. “For” dan keyin esa ot yoki “-ing” keladi: “I use a phone for calling”, “I came here for the course”. Fe’lning o‘zi “for” dan keyin boshlang‘ich shaklda kelmaydi.

Agar maqsaddagi ega boshqa bo‘lsa yoki gapda “can, could, would” kerak bo‘lsa, “so that” ishlatamiz. “I’ll send a map so that you can find us” — xaritani men yuboraman, topadigan esa siz. Ega bir xil bo‘lganda “to” sodda va yaxshi: “I’m saving money to travel”. “So that” dan keyin to‘liq gap keladi: ega va fe’l. O‘tgan zamonda “so that he could” yoki “wouldn’t” deymiz.

Mashq. Uchta savolga maqsadni bildirib javob bering. Birinchi: nima uchun ingliz tilini o‘rganasiz? Ikkinchi: odamlar nima uchun soyabon ishlatadi? Uchinchi: u nima uchun pichirlab gapirdi, masalan chaqaloq uxlab yotgan edi. Har bir javobni “to” yoki “so as not to” bilan boshlang.

Javoblar. Birinchi savolga, masalan, “To get a better job” yoki “To work abroad”. Ikkinchi: “To stay dry”, ya’ni quruq qolish uchun. Uchinchi: “So as not to wake the baby”, chaqaloqni uyg‘otmaslik uchun. Sizning birinchi javobingiz boshqacha bo‘lishi mumkin va bu to‘g‘ri, asosiysi “to” dan keyin fe’l boshlang‘ich shaklda bo‘lsin.

Muhim qoida: predlogdan keyin fe’l doim “-ing” shaklida bo‘ladi, infinitiv emas. “Interested in learning”, “good at cooking”, “afraid of flying”, “thank you for helping”. Alohida diqqat: “look forward to” dagi “to” predlog, shuning uchun “I look forward to seeing you”, “to see” emas. O‘zbek tilida “o‘rganishga qiziqaman” deymiz, ingliz tilida “in” plus “-ing”.

Xato: predlogdan keyin infinitiv qo‘yish. Ayniqsa “I look forward to see you” juda keng tarqalgan. To‘g‘risi “to seeing”. Yodlash usuli: “look forward to” dan keyin “the party” kabi ot qo‘ya olsangiz, demak “to” predlog, fe’l esa “-ing” bo‘ladi. “Look forward to the party” to‘g‘ri, shuning uchun “look forward to seeing” ham to‘g‘ri. Xuddi shunday “be used to doing” — qilishga o‘rganib qolgan bo‘lmoq.

Mashq. Qavs ichidagi fe’lni to‘g‘ri shaklga qo‘ying. Birinchi: “She’s afraid of, drive”. Ikkinchi: “Thank you for, invite, me”. Uchinchi: “I’m looking forward to, meet, your family”. Predlog bor, demak qanday shakl kerak? To‘liq gapni ayting. Uchinchi gapda “to” ni predlog deb eslang va xotirjam o‘ylang.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “afraid of driving”. Ikkinchi: “thank you for inviting me”. Uchinchi: “looking forward to meeting your family”. Uchalasida ham predlog bor va shuning uchun fe’l “-ing” shaklida. Uchinchi gap ayniqsa ko‘pchilikni chalg‘itadi, shuning uchun “to” ni predlog deb eslang.

Keyingi qolip: fe’l, keyin kim, keyin infinitiv. “Want, ask, tell, allow, would like, advise, expect” shu qolipda keladi: “My boss wants me to work late”. O‘zbekcha “mening ishlashimni xohlaydi” deymiz, ingliz tilida “that” gapi emas. Ikkita istisno: “make” va “let” dan keyin “to” tushadi: “She made me laugh”, “He let me go”. Va “want that” deb bo‘lmaydi.

Bu xato rus va o‘zbek tilidan keladi: “Я хочу, чтобы ты помог” — “Men sen yordam berishingni xohlayman”. Inglizcha “I want that you help me” noto‘g‘ri. To‘g‘risi “I want you to help me”. “Make” dan keyin ham “to” qo‘shmang: “She made me to cry” emas, “She made me cry”. “Let” ham xuddi shunday. Lekin passivda “I was made to wait” deymiz, bu keyingi bosqichlar mavzusi.

Mashq. Berilgan so‘zlardan to‘g‘ri gap tuzing. Birinchi: “My mother wants, I, call her”. Ikkinchi: “The teacher told, us, open our books”. Uchinchi: “Dad let, me, drive his car”. “I” ni “me” ga o‘zgartirishni unutmang. Uchinchisida “to” kerakmi? O‘ylab ko‘ring, keyin to‘liq gapni baland ovozda ayting.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “My mother wants me to call her”, “I” emas, “me”. Ikkinchi: “The teacher told us to open our books”. Uchinchi: “Dad let me drive his car”, bu yerda “to” yo‘q. Oxirgisini to‘g‘ri aytgan bo‘lsangiz, istisnani o‘zlashtirgansiz. Birinchi gapda “I” o‘rniga “me” kelganini ham unutmang.

Sifatdan keyin ham infinitiv keladi: “easy to forget”, “happy to help”, “glad to see you”. Yana ikki foydali qolip. “Too plus sifat plus to” — haddan tashqari, shuning uchun mumkin emas: “I’m too tired to cook”. “Sifat plus enough plus to” — yetarli: “He isn’t old enough to drive”. Diqqat: “enough” sifatdan keyin keladi, oldin emas: “old enough”, “enough old” emas.

Birinchi tinglash dialogi. Ikki do‘st, Anna va Ben, dam olish kunlari haqida gaplashadi. Ben buvi-bobosinikiga borishni rejalashtirgan. Tinglang: u bu yerga qanday boradi, nima uchun va Anna unga nimani taklif qiladi? Dialogda infinitiv va gerund qoliplarini ushlashga urining.

Savollarga javob bering. Birinchi: Ben nima qilishni rejalashtiryapti? Ikkinchi: u nima uchun mashina haydashni yoqtirmaydi? Uchinchi: Anna uni nimani esda tutishga undaydi? Javoblarni to‘liq gap bilan, to‘g‘ri qolip bilan ayting. Dialogni yana bir marta ichingizda eslab ko‘ring.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “He’s planning to visit his grandparents”, “plan” infinitiv bilan keladi. Ikkinchi: yo‘l uch soat davom etadi, “It takes three hours”. Uchinchi: “He should remember to book a ticket early”, bu kelajakdagi vazifa, shuning uchun “remember to”. Dialogda “thinking of taking”, ya’ni predlogdan keyin gerund ham bor edi, payqadingizmi?

Ikkinchi dialog ofisda bo‘ladi. Mariya printer buzilgani uchun Tomdan yordam so‘raydi. Tinglang: Tom nimani sinab ko‘rishni maslahat beradi va natija qanday? Diqqat bilan eshiting, qaysi fe’llardan keyin “to”, qaysilaridan keyin “-ing” kelayotganini.

Savollar. Birinchi: Mariya birinchi nimani sinab ko‘rdi? Ikkinchi: u nimani qilishni unutdi? Uchinchi: “used to dealing” dagi “to” predlogmi yoki infinitiv belgisi, va undan keyin qanday shakl keladi? To‘liq javob bering. Oxirgi savol grammatik, shuning uchun qoidani eslang.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “She tried restarting it”, ya’ni sinab ko‘rdi, shuning uchun gerund. Ikkinchi: “She forgot to put paper in”, qog‘oz solishni unutdi. Uchinchi: “be used to” dagi “to” predlog, shuning uchun “-ing” keladi: “used to dealing”. Esda tuting: “I used to play” — oldin o‘ynardim, bunda “to” infinitiv belgisi, ma’no butunlay boshqa.

Talaffuz haqida. Gapda “to” kuchsiz aytiladi: “tu” emas, qisqa “tə”, ya’ni “schwa” bilan. “I want to go” tez aytilganda “wonta go” kabi eshitiladi. Amerika ingliz tilida bu “wanna” deb ham yoziladi, “going to” esa “gonna”. Bu so‘zlashuv shakllari, rasmiy yozuvda ularni ishlatmang. Faqat tinglaganda tanib olish uchun biling. Kuchsiz “to” sizning nutqingizni tabiiy qiladi.

Ba’zan bir gapda bir necha qolip uchraydi. “I decided to stop eating late”: “decide” infinitiv oladi, “stop” esa gerund bilan ma’no beradi. Tushunish uchun gapni bo‘laklarga ajrating: har bir fe’l va undan keyingi shakl alohida juft. Shunday qilsangiz, uzun gap ham qiyin ko‘rinmaydi. Ingliz tilida bunday zanjir tabiiy: “She hopes to start learning to drive”.

Uchta gapda xato bor. Birinchi: “I enjoy to swim in the sea”. Ikkinchi: “We went there for to see the museum”. Uchinchi: “She suggested to take a taxi”. Har bir xatoni toping, sababini ayting va to‘g‘ri variantni baland ovozda ayting. Fe’lning qaysi guruhga kirishini eslang.

Javoblar. Birinchi: “I enjoy swimming”, chunki “enjoy” gerund bilan keladi. Ikkinchi: “We went there to see the museum”, maqsadda “for to” bo‘lmaydi. Uchinchi: “She suggested taking a taxi”, chunki “suggest” gerund oladi. Uchalasini to‘g‘ri topgan bo‘lsangiz, bugungi eng keng tarqalgan xatolardan qutuldingiz.

Qoliplarni qanday yodlaymiz? Birinchi, yangi fe’lni lug‘atga alohida emas, qolibi bilan yozing: “enjoy plus -ing”, “decide plus to”. Ikkinchi, har bir fe’lga o‘zingizga tegishli bitta gap tuzing: “I enjoy cooking”. Uchinchi, guruhlarga ajrating: bir ustunda infinitiv, boshqasida gerund. Yaxshi lug‘atlar, masalan Oxford yoki Cambridge, fe’l qolibini ko‘rsatadi, shuni tekshirib turing.

Endi sizning navbatingiz. O‘zingiz haqingizda to‘rtta gap ayting: bittasi “I want to”, bittasi “I enjoy”, bittasi maqsad, ya’ni “I’m learning English to”, va bittasi “I’m looking forward to” bilan. Bu mashq sizning shaxsiy javobingiz, shuning uchun noto‘g‘ri javob yo‘q. Faqat qolipni to‘g‘ri tanlang. To‘xtang va baland ovozda gapiring.

Mana namuna javoblar. “I want to travel abroad.” “I enjoy reading in the evening.” “I’m learning English to get a better job.” “I’m looking forward to meeting new people.” Sizniki boshqacha bo‘lishi mumkin. Tekshirish juda oson: “want” dan keyin “to”, “enjoy” dan keyin “-ing”, maqsadda “to”, “looking forward to” dan keyin “-ing”. Hammasi mos kelsa, ajoyib.

Xulosa. Birinchi: “want, decide, hope, plan” kabi fe’llardan keyin infinitiv keladi. Ikkinchi: “enjoy, finish, avoid, suggest, mind” kabi fe’llardan keyin “-ing”. Uchinchi: “remember, stop, try” ikkala shakl bilan ma’no o‘zgartiradi. To‘rtinchi: maqsadni “to”, “in order to”, “so as not to” yoki “so that” bilan ifodalaymiz, “for to” deb bo‘lmaydi. Beshinchi: predlogdan keyin faqat “-ing”. Oltinchi: “want you to”, “let me do”.

Uy vazifasi: o‘nta gap yozing va ularda o‘nta turli fe’l qolipidan foydalaning. Masalan, “want to”, “enjoy plus -ing”, “remember to”, “stop plus -ing”, maqsad, “want plus kim plus to”, “let”, “afraid of plus -ing”, “too plus sifat plus to” va “look forward to”. Muvaffaqiyat mezoni: o‘nta gapning hammasida fe’l shakli to‘g‘ri va qoliplar takrorlanmaydi. Keyingi darsda eng kerakli o‘ttizta barqaror iborani o‘rganamiz.
