Ingliz tili. 86-dars. Ko‘chirma gap: to‘liq tizim

Assalomu alaykum! Oldingi darsda matnni tez o‘qishning skimming va scanning usullarini o‘rgandik. Bugun boshqa kuchli ko‘nikma: birovning gapini aniq va tabiiy ko‘chirish. Dars oxirida siz hisobot fe’llarining naqshlarini bilasiz, savol, buyruq va taklifni to‘g‘ri ko‘chirasiz, zamon qachon o‘zgarmasligini tushunasiz hamda suhbatni qisqa xulosa qilib bera olasiz.

Ko‘chirma gap ikki qismdan iborat: hisobot qismi, masalan “she said”, va ko‘chirilgan qism. O‘zbekchada ko‘chirma gap ko‘pincha zamonni o‘zgartirmaydi, “u charchadim dedi” deymiz. Inglizchada esa hisobot fe’li o‘tgan zamonda bo‘lsa, ko‘chirilgan qismdagi zamon odatda bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi. Bu backshift deyiladi.

Jadvalni eslang. Hozirgi oddiy o‘tgan oddiyga o‘tadi: “I work” bu “she worked”. Hozirgi mukammal va o‘tgan oddiy ikkalasi ham o‘tgan mukammalga: “I have finished” va “I finished” bu “she had finished”. Will bu would, can bu could, may bu might. Majburiyat bildiruvchi must esa had to bo‘ladi. “Must” taxmin bildirsa, o‘zgarmaydi.

Zamon bilan birga vaqt va joy so‘zlari ham o‘zgaradi, chunki gapni boshqa paytda va boshqa joyda aytyapsiz. Now bu then, today bu that day, tomorrow bu the next day, yesterday bu the day before. Here bu there, this bu that. Last week bu the week before, ago bu before.

Uch xato. Birinchi: “said me”. Say dan keyin shaxs kelmaydi, tell dan keyin esa shaxs shart: “told me”. Yoki “said to me”. Ikkinchi: o‘tgan zamondagi hisobot fe’lidan keyin zamonni surmaslik, masalan “she said she is tired”. Uchinchi: olmoshni o‘zgartirmaslik, ya’ni “I” ni hamma vaqt “I” qoldirish.

Uchta gapni ko‘chirma gapga aylantiring. Birinchi: Tom ertaga ishlayman dedi. Ikkinchi: ular kalitlarni yo‘qotdik dedi. Uchinchi: u kela olmayman deb menga aytdi. Zamon, olmosh va vaqt so‘zini o‘zgartirishni unutmang. Baland ovozda ayting.

Birinchi: Tom said he was working the next day, bu yerda am working bu was working, tomorrow bu the next day. Ikkinchi: they said they had lost the keys, hozirgi mukammal o‘tgan mukammalga o‘tdi. Uchinchi: she told me she couldn’t come, can’t bu couldn’t. Hammasi jadvalga to‘g‘ri keldi. Endi eng qiziq qism: “said” dan boshqa fe’llar.

Birinchi naqsh: fe’l, keyin that bilan boshlanuvchi gap. That ko‘pincha tushib qoladi. Bunga admit, claim, complain, explain, mention, point out, add, insist, deny kiradi. Har birining o‘z ohangi bor. Admit tan olish, claim bahsli da’vo qilish, complain shikoyat qilish, point out esa e’tiborni qaratish.

Ikkinchi naqsh: fe’l, odam va to-infinitiv. Bu buyruq, maslahat, ogohlantirish fe’llari: advise, ask, invite, order, persuade, remind, tell, warn, encourage, urge. Odam albatta kerak: “She advised me to rest.” Inkor esa to dan oldin not bilan: “He told us not to wait.” O‘zbekchada bunday qurilma yo‘q, shuning uchun uni gap shakli sifatida yod oling.

Uchinchi naqsh: odamsiz to-infinitiv. Bunda faqat gapiruvchining o‘z harakati ko‘chiriladi: promise, refuse, offer, agree, threaten, demand, decide. “She refused to help.” “He offered to drive us.” Ko‘p hollarda that gap bilan ham aytish mumkin: “He promised to call” yoki “He promised that he would call.” Ma’nosi bir xil, birinchisi ixchamroq.

To‘rtinchi naqsh: -ing shakli. Admit, deny, suggest, recommend va mention dan keyin to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri -ing keladi: “He denied taking the money.” Boshqa fe’llar predlog talab qiladi, predlogdan keyin ham -ing: apologise for, accuse somebody of, insist on, thank somebody for, congratulate somebody on. “She apologised for being late.” Bu yerda to-infinitiv ishlatib bo‘lmaydi.

O‘zbek tilida “maslahat berdi, tushuntirdi, uzr so‘radi” fe’llari odamni to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri qabul qiladi, shuning uchun xato ko‘p. Suggest, explain, apologise, describe, mention dan keyin to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri odam kelmaydi, oldin “to” kerak. Suggest dan keyin “to go” ham bo‘lmaydi.

To‘rtta gap. Birinchi: men sizni uyga olib boraman, bu taklif. Ikkinchi: unga tegma, bu ogohlantirish. Uchinchi: kechikkanim uchun kechirasiz, bu uzr. To‘rtinchi: men olmadim, bu rad etish. Mos hisobot fe’lini va shaklini ayting.

Birinchi: he offered to drive me home. Ikkinchi: she warned me not to touch it. Ogohlantirish uchun warned yoki told ham bo‘ladi, lekin warned aniqroq. Uchinchi: he apologised for being late. To‘rtinchi: she denied taking it. Ko‘ryapsizmi, to‘g‘ri fe’l tanlash butun gapni ravshan qiladi, “said” esa hech qachon bunchalik aniq emas.

Savolni ko‘chirganda tartib oddiy darak gapga qaytadi. Inversiya yo‘q, do/does/did yo‘q, so‘roq belgisi ham yo‘q. Yes-no savoli if yoki whether bilan boshlanadi: “She asked if I had finished.” Wh-savol so‘roq so‘zini saqlaydi: “She asked where I lived.” Hisobot fe’llari: asked, wanted to know, wondered.

Uchta savolni ko‘chiring, “She asked…” bilan boshlang. Birinchi: ochmisiz. Ikkinchi: poyezd soat nechada jo‘naydi. Uchinchi: o‘yinni ko‘rdingizmi. Tartibga e’tibor bering: so‘roq so‘zidan keyin ega, keyin fe’l, va zamonni orqaga suring.

Birinchi: she asked if I was hungry, bu yerda am bu was. Ikkinchi: she asked what time the train left. Does yo‘qoldi, leaves bu left. Uchinchi: she asked if I had seen the match, o‘tgan oddiy o‘tgan mukammalga. Hammasida darak gap tartibi. Xatolarning eng ko‘pi: “she asked what time did the train leave”, uni bundan keyin eshitsangiz, tuzating.

Ko‘chirilgan savolda eng ko‘p uchraydigan xato: asl savoldagi tartibni saqlab qolish. Ya’ni so‘roq so‘zidan keyin did yoki do qo‘yish, yoki yes-no savolida if ni tushirib qoldirish. Ko‘chirmada savol darak gapga aylanadi, shuning uchun yordamchi fe’l ham, so‘roq belgisi ham kerak emas. Yes-no savolida if yoki whether albatta bo‘lsin.

Buyruq va iltimosni ko‘chirishda fe’l, odam va to-infinitiv: “Sit down!” bu “He told me to sit down”. Iltimos asked bilan: “Could you open the window?” bu “She asked me to open the window.” Taklif esa suggest bilan: “Let’s go!” bu “He suggested going” yoki “He suggested that we go”. Rasmiy Britaniya ingliz tilida “should go” ham ishlatiladi: “suggested that we should go”.

Uch gapni ko‘chiring. Birinchi: kechikma, told bilan. Ikkinchi: taksi olsak-chi, suggested bilan. Uchinchi: menga yordam bera olasizmi, asked bilan. Birinchisida not to, ikkinchisida -ing yoki that gap kerak. Baland ovozda ayting.

Birinchi: he told me not to be late. Not to, ya’ni inkor to dan oldin. Ikkinchi: she suggested taking a taxi yoki she suggested that we take a taxi. Uchinchisi: he asked me to help him. Diqqat: iltimosda “could you” so‘zi tushib qoladi, uni ko‘chirmaymiz, faqat asked + odam + to. Bu jumlani yodlab oling.

Uchta asosiy fe’l: say, tell va ask. Say dan keyin shaxs to‘g‘ridan-to‘g‘ri kelmaydi, kerak bo‘lsa to bilan: said to me. Tell dan keyin esa shaxs shart: told me. Ask savol yoki iltimos uchun. Ba’zi birikmalar o‘zgarmaydi: say hello, tell the truth, tell a lie, tell a story.

Backshift har doim shart emas. Birinchi: hisobot fe’li hozirgi zamonda bo‘lsa, zamon o‘zgarmaydi: “She says she’s tired.” Ikkinchi: gap hozir ham to‘g‘ri bo‘lsa, ixtiyoriy: “He said the Earth goes round the Sun.” Uchinchi: would, could, should, might, ought to allaqachon o‘tgan shaklda. To‘rtinchi: o‘tgan mukammal yana bir pog‘ona orqaga ketmaydi.

Ikki xil xato uchraydi. Birinchisi: ortiqcha surish, ya’ni o‘tgan mukammalni yana o‘zgartirishga urinish, yoki would va should ni qayta o‘tgan shaklga o‘tkazish. Bunday shakl yo‘q, ular o‘zgarmaydi. Ikkinchi: yaqinda aytilgan va hali dolzarb gapda ham surishni aralashtirish. Vaziyatga qarang: ma’lumot hali dolzarbmi?

To‘rtta gapda zamon suriladimi yoki yo‘qmi, ayting, va ko‘chirma shaklini bering. Birinchisida hisobot fe’li hozirgi. Ikkinchisida fan qonuni. Uchinchisida majburiyat bildiruvchi must. To‘rtinchisida could. Har biri uchun sababni ham qisqa ayting.

Birinchi: says dan keyin zamon o‘zgarmaydi, he is 25. Ikkinchi: fan qonuni hali ham to‘g‘ri, shuning uchun boils ham, boiled ham to‘g‘ri, tabiiyrog‘i boils. Uchinchi: majburiyat must bu had to: she said she had to leave. To‘rtinchi: could o‘zgarmaydi. Bu qoidalarni biling: ular ko‘chirma gapdagi xatolarning katta qismini oldini oladi.

Birinchi tinglash dialogi. Rahbar Ms Clarke xodimi Leo bilan hisobot haqida gaplashyapti. Tinglang va keyin har bir gapni ko‘chirma gap bilan aytib berishga tayyor bo‘ling. Kim nimani so‘radi, nima sababni aytdi, nimani buyurdi va nimaga va’da berdi, shunga e’tibor bering.

Dialogni ko‘chirma gap bilan ayting. Birinchi: Ms Clarke Leodan nimani so‘radi? Ikkinchi: Leo ma’lumotlar va diagrammalar haqida nima dedi? Uchinchi: rahbar unga nima qilishni buyurdi? To‘rtinchi: Leo nimaga va’da berdi? Har bir javobda to‘g‘ri zamon va fe’l naqshini ishlating.

Birinchi: she asked him if he could finish the report by Friday. Ikkinchi: he said the data had arrived late and he hadn’t started the charts, o‘tgan oddiy va hozirgi mukammal ikkalasi o‘tgan mukammalga. Uchinchi: she told him to send Priya the files that day, today bu that day. To‘rtinchi: he promised to check everything twice.

Suhbatni xulosa qilganda har bir gapni takrorlamaymiz. Fikrlarni guruhlab, funksiyasini beruvchi fe’llardan foydalanamiz: ular mavzuni muhokama qildi, bir qarorga keldi, muhokama qilishdi, rozi bo‘lishmadi. Mana foydali iboralar: “They discussed whether to…”, “They agreed on…”, “She objected that…”, “In the end, they decided to…”.

Ikkinchi dialog. Ikki do‘st, Sam va Dina, yozgi ta’tilni rejalashtiryapti. Tinglang va suhbat oxirida nimaga kelishganini ushlang. Keyingi sahnada siz to‘rtta aniq fe’l bilan bu suhbatni xulosa qilasiz: suggested, complained, offered va agreed. Ularning ma’nolarini hozirdan eslang.

Dialogni to‘rt fe’l bilan xulosa qiling: suggested, complained, offered, agreed. Boshlang: “Sam suggested going to the coast, but…” Esda tuting: suggest dan keyin -ing, complain dan keyin that gap, offer dan keyin to-infinitiv, agree dan keyin to-infinitiv yoki that gap. Uch-to‘rt gap yetarli.

Namuna: “Sam suggested going to the coast, but Dina complained that it was always crowded. Sam offered to book a cheap hotel, and Dina agreed, but suggested going in June.” Ikki gap, to‘rt fe’l, barcha naqshlar to‘g‘ri. Dina’ning “I’d rather go hiking” gapi qisqartirildi, chunki asosiy ma’no shikoyatda.

Og‘zaki nutqda ko‘chirma gap tez aytiladi. Bog‘lovchi that kuchsiz shaklda: “said that” da that aniq “dat” emas, balki qisqa “ðət”. Had va would qisqarib “d” ga aylanadi: “He said he’d come” da “he’d” uzun “i” bilan tugaydi. Asked va told kabi so‘zlar keyingi so‘z bilan bog‘lanib ketadi: “asked if”.

Uch gapni tabiiy tezlikda takrorlang. Kuchsiz that va qisqartmalarga e’tibor bering: he’d finished, she’d call, I’d seen. Hamma so‘zni bir xil urg‘u bilan emas, faqat asosiy so‘zlarni kuchliroq aytasiz: finished, call, seen. Bir necha marta takrorlang va tezlikni oshiring.

Hisobot fe’lini tanlash fikringizni ham bildiradi. Claim bahsli da’voni bildiradi, gap yolg‘on bo‘lishi mumkin: “He claimed he was innocent.” Admit aybni tan olish. Point out neytral va foydali, ayniqsa yozuvda. Insist on, ya’ni turib olish. Say neytral. Yangilik va akademik uslubda bunday fe’llar tabiiy, rasmiyroq esa state va announce.

Eng mos fe’lni tanlang. Birinchi: aslida yig‘ilish uchda, neytral eslatish: point out yoki claim? Ikkinchi: ha, vazani men sindirdim, admit yoki deny? Uchinchi: men bormayman, hech qachon, insist yoki offer? Variantlarni ma’nosiga qarab tanlang va butun gapni ayting.

Birinchi: she pointed out that the meeting was at three. Ikkinchi: he admitted breaking the vase. Admit dan keyin -ing ham, that gap ham kelishi mumkin. Uchinchi: she insisted that she wasn’t going. Insist on not going ham to‘g‘ri. Birinchi gapda claim ham grammatik jihatdan to‘g‘ri, lekin u “aslida buni ishonmayman” degan ma’noni qo‘shib yuboradi, shuning uchun point out yaxshiroq.

Yakuniy topshiriq. Qisqa bahs: Ali forma oilalarga pul tejaydi deydi. Mia forma o‘zini ifoda etishni cheklaydi deydi. Ali formalar zerikarli bo‘lishi mumkinligini tan oladi, lekin maktablar uni saqlashi kerak deydi. Uchta turli hisobot fe’li bilan bahsni ko‘chirib bering, masalan claimed, objected, admitted.

Namuna: “Ali claimed that uniforms saved families money. Mia objected that they limited self-expression. Ali admitted that they could be boring but insisted that schools should keep them.” Tekshiring: save saved ga, limit limited ga surildi, can could ga, should o‘zgarmadi. Uchta turli fe’l, bu yerda say bir marta ham ishlatilmadi.

Xulosa. Zamon bir pog‘ona orqaga suriladi, vaqt, joy va olmosh ham o‘zgaradi. Hisobot fe’llari to‘rt naqshda keladi: that gap, odam va to-infinitiv, to-infinitiv, -ing. Savol darak gap tartibida if yoki whether bilan. Buyruq va iltimos tell yoki ask va to bilan, taklif suggest bilan. Hozirgi hisobot fe’li, hali to‘g‘ri ma’lumot va would, could, should, might da surish yo‘q.

Uy vazifasi: o‘zingiz tanlagan mavzuda, masalan telefon va talabalar yoki shahar transporti haqida ikki kishi o‘rtasidagi bahsni yozing yoki videodan tinglang va uni o‘nta turli hisobot fe’li bilan ko‘chirib bering. Muvaffaqiyat mezoni: o‘nta fe’lning har biri to‘g‘ri naqsh bilan, zamon to‘g‘ri surilgan. Keyingi darsda rasmiy va norasmiy xat yozishni o‘rganamiz.
